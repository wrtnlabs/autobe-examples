# Economic/Political Discussion Board Service Overview

## Executive Summary

The Economic/Political Discussion Board is a simple, focused platform designed for meaningful conversations about economic and political topics. Unlike general social media platforms that prioritize engagement metrics over substance, this discussion board emphasizes thoughtful discourse, respectful debate, and knowledge sharing among users interested in these critical subject areas.

This platform addresses the growing need for specialized discussion spaces where users can engage with complex topics without the noise and distractions of mainstream social media. By providing a clean, straightforward interface with essential features like post creation, commenting, and attachment support, the service creates an environment conducive to productive discussions.

## Business Model

### Why This Service Exists

Current social media platforms often fail to provide adequate environments for serious economic and political discussions. They suffer from several key issues:

- **Algorithmic manipulation**: Content prioritization based on engagement rather than quality
- **Lack of specialization**: General platforms mix serious discussions with entertainment content
- **Poor moderation**: Inconsistent enforcement of discussion guidelines
- **Attachment limitations**: Many platforms restrict file sharing for serious research

This discussion board fills a specific market gap by offering:
- A dedicated space for economic/political discourse
- Simple, predictable content organization
- Robust attachment support for research materials
- Focused moderation tailored to these subject areas

### Revenue Strategy

The platform will initially operate as a free service to build community and establish credibility. Future monetization opportunities include:

- **Premium features**: Enhanced attachment limits, advanced search capabilities
- **Sponsored content**: Carefully curated educational content from reputable sources
- **Enterprise subscriptions**: Custom discussion boards for academic institutions or research organizations
- **Advertising**: Non-intrusive, topic-relevant advertisements

### Growth Plan

**Phase 1 (Launch - 6 months):**
- Focus on organic growth through content quality
- Establish core community guidelines and moderation standards
- Build reputation as a reliable discussion platform

**Phase 2 (6-12 months):**
- Implement premium features for power users

- Expand moderation team based on community needs

**Phase 3 (12+ months):**
- Explore enterprise solutions
- Consider mobile application development
- Expand to related subject areas if community demands

## User Journey Overview

### Registration and Onboarding
WHEN a new user visits the platform, THE system SHALL provide a simple registration process that collects only essential information. THE registration SHALL require email verification to ensure user authenticity.

### Post Creation Flow
WHEN a user creates a post, THE system SHALL provide a clean editor with attachment upload capabilities. THE post creation interface SHALL include category selection and tagging options to organize content effectively.

### Commenting System
WHEN users engage with posts, THE commenting system SHALL support threaded discussions with proper nesting. THE system SHALL provide clear moderation indicators and user reputation displays.

### Attachment Management
WHEN users upload attachments, THE system SHALL support multiple file types including images, PDFs, and documents. THE attachment system SHALL include size limits and virus scanning capabilities.
