# Customer Service and Support Requirements

## Executive Summary

THE customer service system SHALL serve as the central support infrastructure for the shopping mall platform, providing multi-channel customer assistance, ticket management, dispute resolution, and quality assurance capabilities. THE system SHALL integrate seamlessly with existing platform components while maintaining high service standards and customer satisfaction metrics.

WHEN customers encounter issues or require assistance, THE system SHALL provide immediate access to support services through preferred communication channels while maintaining comprehensive interaction history and resolution tracking. THE system SHALL ensure consistent service delivery across all channels and customer segments.

## Customer Support Channels

### Multi-Channel Support Infrastructure

THE platform SHALL provide integrated customer support across multiple channels including email, live chat, phone support, social media monitoring, and messaging platforms. THE system SHALL maintain unified conversation history across all channels, enabling customers to continue conversations seamlessly when switching between communication methods.

WHEN customers initiate contact, THE system SHALL identify optimal support channels based on customer type, issue complexity, current system capacity, and historical interaction preferences. THE system SHALL provide estimated response times and queue positions for each available channel to help customers make informed choices about their support experience.

THE multi-channel system SHALL implement intelligent routing capabilities that consider agent availability, skill matching, language requirements, and current workload distribution to optimize customer-agent pairings. THE system SHALL maintain real-time channel status monitoring with automatic failover capabilities to ensure continuous service availability.

WHERE high-priority customers or urgent issues are identified, THE system SHALL provide priority channel access with reduced wait times and specialized agent assignment. THE system SHALL implement VIP customer identification based on account value, purchase history, or platform contribution levels.

### Email Support Requirements

THE email support system SHALL provide structured ticket creation through dedicated support email addresses with automatic parsing, categorization, and routing capabilities. THE system SHALL implement intelligent email analysis using natural language processing to understand customer intent, urgency levels, and appropriate categorization for efficient handling.

WHEN processing email inquiries, THE system SHALL maintain complete conversation threading with automatic message association based on subject line analysis, sender identification, and content relevance scoring. THE system SHALL support rich text formatting, multiple file attachments, and embedded media handling while maintaining email response templates for common inquiry types.

THE email system SHALL provide automatic acknowledgment responses within configurable timeframes, confirming receipt and providing ticket reference numbers with clear expectations for response timing. THE system SHALL implement priority routing based on keyword detection, sender history analysis, and business rule evaluation to ensure urgent matters receive immediate attention.

WHERE email conversations require escalation or specialized handling, THE system SHALL provide seamless transfer capabilities while preserving complete interaction history and ensuring continuity of customer communication. THE system SHALL maintain audit trails of all email processing activities for quality assurance and compliance purposes.

### Live Chat Support Requirements

THE live chat system SHALL provide real-time communication with queue management, estimated wait times, and position notifications to enhance customer experience during high-demand periods. THE system SHALL implement intelligent chat routing based on agent skills, current availability, language capabilities, and customer tier classifications for optimal pairing effectiveness.

WHEN initiating chat sessions, THE system SHALL collect preliminary information through pre-chat forms including customer identification, issue categorization, urgency assessment, and preferred communication language. THE system SHALL provide contextual information to assigned agents including customer history, recent platform activity, and any relevant account details for personalized service delivery.

WHILE chat sessions are active, THE system SHALL provide real-time communication features including typing indicators, read receipts, file sharing capabilities, and co-browsing functionality when appropriate for issue resolution. THE system SHALL support chat session recording with customer consent for training, quality assurance, and dispute resolution purposes.

THE live chat system SHALL implement proactive chat capabilities allowing agents to initiate conversations with customers showing signs of confusion, extended browsing on support pages, or repeated unsuccessful self-service attempts. THE system SHALL maintain chat availability scheduling with automatic status updates based on agent login patterns and shift management.

### Phone Support Integration

THE phone support system SHALL integrate with modern telephony platforms providing call recording, intelligent routing, interactive voice response capabilities, and comprehensive call logging for customer service tracking. THE system SHALL implement voice recognition and natural language processing for automated call routing and preliminary issue identification before agent assignment.

WHEN customers request phone support, THE system SHALL provide callback scheduling options to eliminate waiting times, allowing customers to request calls at convenient times with confirmation notifications sent through preferred communication channels. THE system SHALL maintain callback queues with priority settings based on customer tier, issue urgency, and historical relationship value.

THE phone support system SHALL provide comprehensive call logging including conversation summaries, issue categorization, resolution status, and follow-up requirements for comprehensive customer interaction tracking. THE system SHALL support conference calling capabilities for complex issues requiring multiple department involvement or third-party participation.

### Social Media and Messaging Platform Support

THE social media monitoring system SHALL continuously scan major social platforms for mentions, direct messages, comments, and reviews related to the shopping mall platform, automatically converting relevant communications into support tickets for proper tracking and resolution. THE system SHALL maintain platform-specific response capabilities while ensuring consistent brand voice and accurate information across all social interactions.

THE messaging platform integration SHALL provide support across popular messaging applications including WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, WeChat, and other regionally significant platforms while maintaining conversation continuity and customer preference tracking. THE system SHALL implement message formatting adaptation for platform-specific requirements and character limitations.

WHEN social media or messaging inquiries are received, THE system SHALL provide rapid response capabilities for public-facing communications to protect brand reputation while maintaining appropriate escalation procedures for complex issues requiring detailed investigation or private information handling. THE system SHALL implement social media sentiment analysis for identifying trending issues or potential crises requiring immediate management attention.

## Ticket Management System

### Automated Ticket Creation and Classification

THE ticket creation system SHALL automatically generate support tickets from all customer inquiries across email, chat, phone, social media, and web portal channels with comprehensive metadata capture and intelligent categorization. THE system SHALL implement machine learning algorithms for issue classification based on content analysis, customer history, and resolution patterns to improve accuracy over time.

WHEN creating tickets, THE system SHALL capture essential information including customer identification, contact details, issue categorization, priority level, source channel, creation timestamp, and initial interaction content. THE system SHALL generate unique ticket identifiers following consistent formatting rules and provide customer-friendly reference numbers for easy communication and tracking.

THE ticket system SHALL support manual ticket creation by support staff for issues reported through non-automated channels, providing comprehensive templates for common scenarios and ensuring consistent information collection across all creation methods. THE system SHALL implement duplicate detection capabilities to identify and merge related tickets while preserving all associated communications and attachments.

### Priority and Categorization Framework

THE categorization system SHALL provide hierarchical classification structures with main categories, subcategories, detailed tags, and resolution type classifications to enable precise issue routing and comprehensive trend analysis. The system SHALL implement dynamic category management allowing administrators to modify classification structures based on evolving business needs and emerging issue patterns.

THE priority classification system SHALL implement four-tier priority levels (Critical, High, Medium, Low) with clear criteria definitions, response time expectations, and resolution targets specific to each category. THE system SHALL provide automatic priority suggestions based on customer tier, issue type, business impact assessment, and historical severity indicators while allowing agent override capabilities with justification requirements.

WHEN determining ticket priority, THE system SHALL consider multiple factors including customer value classification, order or transaction amounts, platform impact scope, time sensitivity, and potential business reputation implications. THE system SHALL maintain priority override controls requiring management approval for deviations from automatic suggestions while preserving audit trails for compliance and performance analysis.

### Assignment and Routing Mechanisms

THE assignment system SHALL implement intelligent routing algorithms matching customer issues with support agent skills, availability, current workload, language capabilities, and specialization areas for optimal resolution efficiency. The system SHALL maintain comprehensive agent profiles including product expertise, technical certifications, communication skills, performance history, and availability schedules to support intelligent assignment decisions.

THE routing system SHALL provide workload balancing capabilities preventing agent overload while ensuring timely response delivery across all support channels and customer segments. THE system SHALL implement automatic redistribution mechanisms when assignment imbalances are detected and provide escalation triggers when response time commitments are at risk due to capacity constraints.

WHERE specialized knowledge is required, THE system SHALL support skill-based routing to technical specialists, product experts, or management personnel with appropriate authorization levels and issue complexity matching. THE system SHALL maintain escalation pathways for complex issues requiring cross-department coordination or third-party involvement.

### State Management and Lifecycle Tracking

THE ticket lifecycle system SHALL provide comprehensive state tracking from initial creation through final resolution with detailed history maintenance for all status changes, assignments, communications, and resolution activities. The system SHALL implement standard states including New, In Progress, Pending Customer, Pending Internal, Resolved, and Closed with configurable transition rules and permission controls.

WHEN tickets change states, THE system SHALL automatically log transition details including timestamps, responsible parties, state change reasons, optional explanatory notes, and system-generated context to maintain complete audit trails. THE system SHALL provide state history visualization and reporting capabilities to support quality assurance reviews and operational analysis requirements.

THE system SHALL implement aging management with configurable time limits for each ticket state, generating automatic notifications and escalation triggers when tickets exceed defined thresholds. THE system SHALL provide aging reports and overdue alerts with management visibility and corrective action workflows to ensure no tickets are neglected during busy periods or resource constraints.

## Escalation Procedures

### Tiered Support Structure Implementation

THE escalation system SHALL implement structured support tiers with clearly defined roles, responsibilities, authorization levels, and expertise requirements appropriate for different issue complexity levels and customer types. The system SHALL define Tier 1 for general support inquiries, Tier 2 for technical issues requiring specialized knowledge, and Tier 3 for complex problems needing management intervention or cross-department coordination.

THE tiered structure SHALL provide seamless escalation pathways allowing smooth transitions between support levels while maintaining customer communication continuity and preserving complete issue context for efficient handling. THE system SHALL implement tier-specific training requirements, performance metrics, and quality standards to ensure consistent service delivery across all support levels.

WHEN escalations occur, THE system SHALL provide comprehensive handoff procedures including detailed issue summaries, attempted resolution steps, escalation justifications, customer communication history, and recommendations for next steps in the resolution process. THE system SHALL maintain escalation time tracking and success metrics to optimize tier performance and identify training opportunities.

### Automatic Escalation Mechanisms

THE automatic escalation system SHALL implement trigger-based escalation using time thresholds, priority levels, customer value classifications, and issue complexity indicators to ensure appropriate resource allocation. The system SHALL provide configurable escalation rules allowing administrators to modify triggers based on SLA requirements, business priorities, and operational capacity constraints.

THE system SHALL generate automatic notifications when escalation triggers are activated, informing appropriate supervisors, higher-tier support staff, and management personnel through preferred communication channels while providing comprehensive context and escalation justification details. THE system SHALL support escalation acknowledgment requirements ensuring escalated issues receive appropriate attention and resource allocation.

WHERE automatic escalations are prevented due to system constraints or policy restrictions, THE system SHALL provide alternative notification mechanisms ensuring management awareness of potential service issues while maintaining escalation attempt records for compliance and performance analysis purposes.

### Manual Escalation Workflows

THE manual escalation system SHALL provide intuitive interfaces enabling support agents to escalate issues requiring management attention, specialized expertise, or additional resources while requiring appropriate justification and supporting documentation. The system SHALL implement escalation approval workflows based on severity levels, business impact assessments, and customer relationship considerations to ensure proper authorization and resource allocation.

THE manual escalation interface SHALL provide template-based justification forms ensuring consistent information collection and comprehensive context documentation for efficient tier transfer and resolution continuity. THE system SHALL maintain escalation request tracking with approval status monitoring, rejection management, and alternative pathway suggestions when escalations cannot be accommodated immediately.

WHEN manual escalations are approved, THE system SHALL provide smooth handoff procedures including ticket reassignment, customer notification, status updates, and expectation management to maintain service quality during transition periods. THE system SHALL support escalation priority assessment allowing managers to evaluate and reprioritize escalated issues based on current workload and business constraints.

## Dispute Resolution

### Comprehensive Dispute Classification

THE dispute resolution system SHALL provide exhaustive categorization covering product quality disputes, delivery problems, payment disagreements, seller conduct issues, platform functionality complaints, and service-related grievances. The system SHALL implement specialized workflows for each dispute type with investigation procedures, documentation requirements, resolution timelines, and customer communication protocols specific to each category.

THE system SHALL support dispute severity assessment considering financial impact, customer relationship value, potential legal implications, and platform reputation risks to determine appropriate investigation depth and resource allocation for each case. THE system SHALL maintain dispute history tracking to identify repeat patterns and implement preventive measures for common issue types.

WHEN disputes are submitted, THE system SHALL immediately secure relevant evidence including transaction records, communication logs, product information, payment details, and delivery documentation to establish investigation baselines and prevent evidence modification or loss during dispute processing periods.

### Investigation and Evidence Management

THE investigation system SHALL provide structured evidence collection processes including document gathering, statement collection, timeline reconstruction, and third-party verification when applicable to ensure comprehensive and fair dispute resolution. The system SHALL implement evidence chain-of-custody procedures maintaining integrity and authenticity throughout the investigation process for potential legal or compliance requirements.

THE investigation workflow SHALL provide neutral fact-finding processes ensuring unbiased evaluation of all parties involved while maintaining detailed activity logs tracking every investigation step, communication attempt, and evidence review activity for transparency and accountability purposes. THE system SHALL support multimedia evidence collection including photos, videos, audio recordings, and digital documents with secure storage and access control mechanisms.

WHILE investigations are ongoing, THE system SHALL maintain regular communication with disputing parties providing status updates, timeline expectations, and process explanations while preserving confidentiality regarding sensitive information or ongoing deliberations. THE system SHALL implement investigation deadline management with automatic escalation when timelines are at risk to ensure timely resolution and customer satisfaction.

### Resolution Framework and Compensation

THE resolution system SHALL provide structured resolution options including full refunds, partial compensation, product replacements, service credits, policy exceptions, and alternative dispute resolution methods based on investigation findings and business relationship considerations. The system SHALL implement compensation approval workflows with monetary thresholds and management authorization requirements to ensure appropriate financial control while enabling timely customer satisfaction recovery.

THE resolution framework SHALL facilitate negotiation processes when parties express willingness to reach mutual agreement, providing structured offer and counteroffer mechanisms with documentation requirements and acceptance tracking for legally binding dispute settlements. THE system SHALL support resolution package creation including compensation details, policy explanations, future prevention measures, and customer education components to maximize dispute resolution value beyond immediate financial considerations.

WHERE disputes involve seller performance issues, THE system SHALL provide seller notification and improvement plan requirements including specific performance expectations, monitoring periods, training recommendations, and potential account action consequences for repeated or serious violations. THE system SHALL maintain seller dispute statistics tracking resolution rates, customer satisfaction scores, and repeat occurrence patterns to support platform quality management initiatives.

### Appeal and Review Processes

THE appeal system SHALL provide independent review mechanisms allowing dissatisfied parties to request second evaluations of dispute resolution decisions through fresh investigation processes with different personnel and expanded evidence consideration. The system SHALL implement appeal deadline management with automatic notifications and scheduling systems to ensure appeal rights are preserved while maintaining timely resolution expectations.

THE review process SHALL provide escalation pathways to senior management, legal department consultation, or third-party mediation services when initial dispute resolution and appeal processes fail to achieve satisfactory outcomes for all parties involved. THE system SHALL maintain comprehensive appeal tracking with outcome statistics, decision rationale documentation, and process improvement recommendations based on appeal patterns and results.

The system SHALL maintain template optimization through regular performance analysis, customer feedback integration, and issue resolution effectiveness measurement to ensure continual improvement in response quality and customer satisfaction.

THE system SHALL implement template approval workflows with subject matter expert review requirements and legal compliance verification when required for sensitive or contract-related communications.

THE system SHALL support template personalization allowing agents to customize standard responses with customer names, specific details, relevant context, and empathetic language while maintaining accuracy, professionalism, and brand voice consistency across all communications. THE system SHALL provide template usage analytics tracking selection rates, personalization frequency, and customer response quality to guide template library optimization efforts.

### Automated Notification Framework

THE notification system SHALL provide comprehensive customer communications throughout ticket lifecycles including creation confirmations, assignment notifications, status updates, resolution announcements, follow-up requests, and satisfaction surveys delivered through preferred communication channels. The system SHALL support multi-channel notifications enabling customers to receive updates via email, SMS, push notifications, in-platform messages, or voice calls based on individual preferences and communication priority levels.

THE automated notification framework SHALL provide intelligent timing controls preventing message flooding while ensuring customers receive timely updates during critical process stages, with frequency management based on communication type, customer preferences, and issue urgency indicators. THE system SHALL implement notification acknowledgment tracking with escalation procedures when critical updates remain unread or unacknowledged beyond defined timeframes.

THE system SHALL support seamless language switching during conversations while maintaining complete conversation history and preventing repetition of previously communicated information.

THE system SHALL maintain language-specific knowledge bases containing localized policy information, regional regulatory requirements, cultural communication preferences, and market-specific product details to ensure accurate and appropriate support delivery across diverse customer populations. THE system SHALL provide translation quality monitoring with customer feedback integration and human review triggers when communication effectiveness metrics indicate potential translation quality issues.

## Knowledge Base and Self-Service Requirements

### Comprehensive Self-Service Capabilities

THE self-service knowledge base SHALL provide exhaustive coverage of common customer questions, platform functionality guidance, policy explanations, troubleshooting procedures, and account management instructions enabling customers to find solutions independently without requiring direct support contact. The system SHALL implement intelligent search capabilities including natural language processing, keyword matching, synonym recognition, and context-aware result ranking to optimize information discovery success rates and self-service effectiveness.

THE knowledge base system SHALL provide multi-format content delivery including text articles, step-by-step tutorials, video demonstrations, interactive guides, and downloadable resources to accommodate different learning preferences and accessibility requirements across diverse customer populations. THE system SHALL implement content difficulty progression allowing customers to access basic, intermediate, and advanced information levels based on their familiarity with platform features and technical comfort levels.

THE self-service system SHALL provide personalization capabilities using customer profiles, purchase history, platform usage patterns, and previous interaction records to present relevant information proactively and customize knowledge delivery based on individual customer context and needs. THE system SHALL implement self-service success tracking with analytics identifying article effectiveness, search success rates, and escalation patterns to guide continuous improvement initiatives and content optimization efforts.

THE content management system SHALL provide comprehensive authoring tools enabling support staff, subject matter experts, and approved contributors to create, review, publish, and maintain knowledge base content with version control, approval workflows, and quality standards enforcement. The system SHALL implement content taxonomy providing hierarchical organization, cross-referencing capabilities, and intuitive navigation structures to optimize content discoverability and maintenance efficiency.

THE knowledge base maintenance system SHALL provide systematic review processes ensuring content accuracy, completeness, and currency through regular audits, user feedback integration, ownership assignments, and change management procedures applicable to all knowledge content. THE system SHALL provide content performance analytics identifying popular articles, search failure patterns, content gaps, and improvement opportunities to guide strategic knowledge base development and resource allocation decisions.

THE search system SHALL provide intelligent information retrieval capabilities including automatic query suggestion, spelling correction, related topic recommendations, and advanced filtering options to enhance user experience and information discovery success rates across all customer segments. The system SHALL implement learning algorithms analyzing search patterns, click-through rates, and resolution success to continuously improve search result relevance and knowledge base organization effectiveness.

THE integration framework SHALL provide real-time data synchronization ensuring all customer service interactions reflect current platform state, recent customer activities, and updated account information to prevent contradictory communications and support staff confusion during customer interactions. THE system SHALL implement integration security protocols protecting sensitive customer information while enabling efficient access for authorized support personnel across all integrated systems and functionalities.

THE system SHALL support third-party integration capabilities with customer relationship management systems, communication platforms, analytics tools, and specialized service applications while maintaining security standards, data protection requirements, and operational continuity throughout all integration activities and platform evolution initiatives.
