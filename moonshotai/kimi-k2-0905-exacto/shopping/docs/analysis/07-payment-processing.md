# Payment Processing Requirements Specification

## Executive Summary  

THE ecommerceMall platform SHALL provide comprehensive payment processing capabilities supporting multiple payment methods while maintaining PCI DSS compliance and enabling secure multi-vendor transaction processing. THE system SHALL handle complex payment scenarios including single orders containing products from multiple sellers, process refunds efficiently, and maintain detailed audit trails for all financial transactions. THE payment infrastructure SHALL support payment authorization within 3 seconds, maintain 99.9% uptime measured monthly, and process refunds within 3-5 business days while providing transparent financial reporting for all stakeholders.

## Payment Method Support

### Primary Payment Methods  

**THE system SHALL support credit and debit card processing** including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover networks. WHEN a customer selects credit card payment, THE system SHALL validate card number format (Luhn algorithm), verify expiration dates, validate CVV codes, and confirm billing address through Address Verification Service (AVS). THE system SHALL display clear error messages when payment validation fails, providing specific guidance about required corrections. IF credit card payment is declined due to insufficient funds, THEN THE system SHALL notify the customer immediately and offer alternative payment method selection without requiring re-entry of order information. THE system SHALL support saved payment methods for returning customers with secure tokenization preventing storage of actual card numbers on platform servers.  

**THE platform SHALL integrate digital wallet payments** including PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Samsung Pay. WHEN customers choose digital wallet payment, THE system SHALL initiate secure mobile payment flows appropriate to each wallet ecosystem. THE system SHALL verify wallet payment availability for the customer's device and operating system combination. WHERE digital wallet payment fails, THE system SHALL seamlessly fall back to alternative payment methods while preserving the customer's cart contents. THE system SHALL display wallet payment branding prominently during checkout to build customer confidence in security measures. 

**THE system SHALL support bank transfer payments** through ACH processing for customers preferring direct bank payments. WHEN bank transfer is selected, THE system SHALL provide clear instructions for bank account verification including micro-deposit confirmation processes where required. THE system SHALL calculate estimated processing times for bank transfers ranging from 3-5 business days for standard ACH to same-day for wire transfers. IF bank transfer payment is initiated but funds are not confirmed within specified timeframes, THEN THE system SHALL automatically cancel the order and notify the customer of cancellation reasons. THE system SHALL maintain bank account security through encrypted storage and limited access protocols.  

**THE platform SHALL offer alternative payment methods** including Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) options and cryptocurrency processing as business requirements dictate. WHEN alternative payment methods are selected, THE system SHALL provide clear explanation of payment terms including interest rates, repayment schedules, and any associated fees. THE system SHALL validate customer eligibility for credit-based payment alternatives through integrated credit checking mechanisms. WHERE alternative payment methods require additional verification steps, THE system SHALL guide customers through verification processes while maintaining security requirements. THE system SHALL support regional payment method preferences based on customer location and local market requirements.  

### Multi-Currency Processing

**WHERE the platform supports international transactions, THE system SHALL process payments in the customer's local currency based on detected geographic location or customer preference settings.** When currency selection differs from seller's preferred settlement currency, THE system SHALL calculate exchange rates using real-time market data and display conversion rates transparently to customers. THE system SHALL maintain transaction records in both the customer's payment currency and the seller's settlement currency for accurate accounting and reconciliation. IF exchange rates change significantly between order placement and payment processing, THEN THE system SHALL provide clear notification to customers about conversion rate differences and confirmation options. THE system SHALL support automatic currency conversion for refund processing ensuring customers receive appropriate amounts in their original payment currency regardless of timing or market fluctuations. THE platform SHALL generate tax reports and financial summaries in appropriate currencies for seller accounting requirements.

## Transaction Security Standards

### PCI DSS Compliance Requirements

**THE system SHALL maintain PCI DSS Level 1 compliance status across all payment processing infrastructure including secure network configuration, data encryption, access control, and regular security monitoring requirements.** THE system SHALL implement network segmentation ensuring payment processing networks are isolated from general platform networks through firewall configurations and intrusion detection systems. THE system SHALL conduct quarterly vulnerability scans and annual penetration testing by qualified security assessors to maintain compliance certification. WHERE payment card data is processed, THE system SHALL enforce strict access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel through role-based permissions and multi-factor authentication requirements. IFCardholder data is encountered during transaction processing, THEN THE system SHALL immediately encrypt the data using approved encryption algorithms (AES-256 minimum) and store only essential transaction records that do not contain full card numbers. THE system SHALL maintain audit logs of all payment system access events for minimum one year with secure storage and access controls preventing modification of historical records.  

**THE payment processing infrastructure SHALL implement comprehensive data protection measures including encryption at rest for database storage, encryption in transit for all network communications, and tokenization for payment method storage.** THE system SHALL generate secure tokens that replace actual card numbers in customer account profiles providing customers with saved payment method convenience while maintaining security standards. THE system SHALL implement certificate pinning for payment gateway communications preventing man-in-the-middle attacks through forged certificates. WHEN payment tokens are stored in customer profiles, THE system SHALL implement restrictions preventing token use for transactions other than the originating merchant and approved transaction types. THE system SHALL maintain encryption key rotation schedules ensuring long-term security of stored payment data through regular key replacement protocols.  

**THE fraud prevention system SHALL monitor payment transactions using machine learning algorithms to identify suspicious patterns while maintaining a false positive rate below 5% to prevent legitimate transaction rejection.** THE system SHALL implement velocity checking analyzing the number of transactions attempted from single IP addresses, specific card numbers, or shipping addresses within specified time windows. THE system SHALL maintain fraud scoring for each transaction based on multiple factors including geographic consistency, device fingerprinting, transaction amount patterns, and historical customer behavior analysis. WHERE transactions are flagged for manual review, THE system SHALL queue suspicious transactions while allowing normal transactions to continue processing without significant delays. THE system SHALL support geographic validation ensuring IP address locations match billing address locations within reasonable parameters and flagging anomalies for investigation.  

### Authentication and Validation Protocols

**THE payment processing system SHALL implement 3D Secure authentication for card payments when supported by card issuers providing additional verification through one-time passwords or biometric authentication.** WHEN 3D Secure authentication is triggered, THE system SHALL seamlessly redirect customers to issuer authentication pages while maintaining transaction context and shopping cart contents. THE system SHALL support Strong Customer Authentication requirements for European transactions under PSD2 regulations including two-factor authentication requirements for payments exceeding specified thresholds or when other risk factors are present. IF 3D Secure authentication fails or is declined by the customer, THEN THE system SHALL provide clear error messaging explaining authentication failure reasons and offering alternative payment method selection. THE system SHALL maintain extensible authentication architecture supporting future authentication methods while ensuring compatibility with existing customer experiences and seller integration requirements.  

## Payment Processing Flow

### Standard Payment Authorization

**WHEN a customer initiates a payment transaction, THE system SHALL complete the entire validation and authorization process within 3 seconds from submission to confirmation display excluding bank processing time delays beyond platform control.** THE authorization flow SHALL begin with payment method validation including card number format verification using Luhn algorithm, expiration date validation ensuring the card has not expired, CVV code validation for length and format compliance, and billing address verification against customer account information. THE system SHALL support Address Verification Service (AVS) checking where the numeric portions of billing addresses are verified against card issuer records providing additional fraud prevention confidence while maintaining customer privacy. WHERE payment method validation succeeds, THE system SHALL request payment authorization from the appropriate payment gateway while preserving transaction context including order details, customer information, and seller allocation requirements.  

**THE payment authorization SHALL calculate appropriate fees, taxes, and shipping costs for the complete transaction while handling multi-seller order allocation ensuring each seller receives proper payment amounts.** WHEN processing orders containing products from multiple sellers, THE system SHALL create separate payment records for each seller's portion while displaying unified payment confirmation to customers. THE system SHALL automatically deduct platform commission percentages according to individual seller agreements and product category fee structures. IF payment authorization succeeds for one seller's products but fails for another seller's products, THEN THE system SHALL provide customers with options to remove failing products and retry payment or select alternative payment methods while preserving successfully authorized portions. THE system SHALL implement idempotent authorization requests preventing duplicate payment charges through transaction-specific identifier generation and verification.  

**WHERE payment authorization is successfully completed through payment gateway providers, THE system SHALL immediately capture authorized amounts and begin order fulfillment processes while providing customers with immediate confirmation of successful payment processing.** THE capture process SHALL trigger automatic order status updates through the order management system ensuring sellers receive notifications and can begin fulfillment processes without delays. THE system SHALL generate comprehensive payment confirmation emails to customers containing transaction details, seller breakdown, and order tracking information accessible through customer account dashboards. THE system SHALL maintain payment confirmation receipts in customer account profiles providing permanent records of transactions with ability to export payment details for personal accounting or reimbursement processing.  

### Transaction Status Management and Updates

**THE payment system SHALL maintain real-time transaction status tracking with automatic updates accessible through customer account dashboards and provided to sellers through their management interfaces.** Transaction status SHALL progress through clearly defined stages including Pending (payment initiated but not processed), Authorized (payment approved by gateway but funds not captured), Captured (payment completed successfully and order processing initiated), Failed (payment declined or technical errors encountered), Refunded (full payment returned to customer), Chargeback (customer dispute initiated with card issuer), and Partially Refunded (partial refund processed for returned items or order modifications). WHEN payment status changes occur, THE system SHALL immediately notify affected parties through appropriate communication channels including email notifications, push notifications for mobile applications, and dashboard updates for seller management interfaces.  

**THE system SHALL implement comprehensive audit trail functionality logging all payment-related activities with timestamps, user identification, transaction amounts, payment methods used, and specific actions performed.** Audit logs SHALL be maintained for minimum 7 years providing complete transaction histories for regulatory compliance, accounting reconciliation, and dispute resolution requirements. THE audit trail SHALL include payment initiation timestamps, authorization response details, capture confirmation records, refund processing events, and any payment failure or error explanations for comprehensive transaction analysis. Where payment disputes require investigation, THE system SHALL provide detailed audit information to authorized administrators while maintaining appropriate access controls preventing unauthorized disclosure of individual customer payment details.  

## Refund and Reversal Processing

### Refund Authorization and Processing

**THE system SHALL support comprehensive refund processing covering full order cancellations, partial cancellations for specific items, product returns resulting in customer reimbursements, and seller-initiated refunds for customer service satisfaction improvement.** WHEN processing refunds, THE system SHALL automatically calculate appropriate refund amounts including adjustments for individual product prices, applicable sales taxes, shipping costs where not utilized, platform commission adjustments, and any promotional discounts originally applied to specific items being refunded. THE system SHALL generate refund requests through appropriate payment gateway interfaces ensuring customers receive refunds through their original payment methods where supported by payment provider policies. IF original payment methods cannot receive refunds due to expired cards or closed accounts, THEN THE system SHALL provide alternative arrangement options including bank transfers, paper checks processed through accounting systems, or alternative digital payment methods according to customer preferences and business policy requirements.  

**The refund processing workflow SHALL support automated procedures for transaction adjustment scenarios including order modifications that reduce total order value, promotional pricing corrections that result in customer overpayment refunds, shipping cost corrections where delivery failures require shipping fee reimbursements, and product quality issues that justify seller-initiated goodwill refunds maintaining customer relationships.** THE refund processing system SHALL handle tax jurisdictions appropriately by identifying applicable tax rates at original transaction time and calculating refund amounts with identical tax computations ensuring accounting accuracy for both platform and individual seller financial reporting. THE system SHALL maintain detailed refund history for each customer providing visibility into overall refund patterns while protecting individual transaction confidentiality and preventing unauthorized access.  

### Chargeback Management and Dispute Resolution

**THE system SHALL implement comprehensive chargeback management processes protecting seller interests while ensuring proper documentation and evidence submission for contested chargeback scenarios.** WHEN chargebacks are received through payment processor notifications, THE system SHALL immediately place affected transaction funds in holding status preventing automatic payouts to sellers while preserving fund availability for potential recovery requirements. THE system SHALL compile comprehensive evidence packages for chargeback responses including transaction receipts, delivery confirmation details, customer communication history, and return policy documentation where applicable for dispute arbitration purposes. THE system SHALL track chargeback ratios by individual seller providing early warning alerts when seller chargeback volumes exceed acceptable thresholds where typical acceptable rates remain below 1% of total transaction volume.  

**THE chargeback processing system SHALL support manual review procedures where experienced administrators evaluate chargeback legitimacy while working with sellers to compile appropriate response documentation including customer communication records, product delivery confirmations, and relevant policy documentation supporting dispute resolution.** IF chargeback disputes are successfully resolved in seller favor, THEN THE system SHALL automatically release held funds and provide clear documentation of resolution outcomes with appropriate classification for seller record maintenance. THE system SHALL maintain detailed chargeback history analysis enabling platform-wide fraud pattern detection and seller education programs addressing common causes of customer chargeback initiation including product description accuracy, shipping timeline consistency, and customer service responsiveness improvement opportunities.  

## Financial Reporting and Seller Settlement

### Transaction Reporting and Analytics

**THE payment reporting system SHALL provide comprehensive transaction analytics to sellers including daily sales summaries broken down by payment method, weekly performance comparisons with previous periods, monthly revenue analysis with growth trend identification, and annual financial summaries supporting tax preparation requirements.** Reporting SHALL include detailed breakdown of all transaction fees charged during payment processing including credit card interchange fees, digital wallet processing charges, international processing premiums, and tax compliance service fees where applicable. THE system SHALL support customizable reporting periods allowing merchants to select specific date ranges for performance analysis including holiday season analysis, promotional campaign impact evaluation, and inventory liquidation effectiveness assessment. WHEN sellers access payment reporting functionality, THE system SHALL provide quick visualizations including charts showing payment method preference evolution over time, revenue growth trend lines with seasonality identification, and transaction volume comparisons for customer behavior analysis purposes.  

**THE settlement reporting system SHALL calculate accurate seller earnings by determining gross transaction amounts, subtracting platform commission fees according to individual seller agreements, removes applicable payment processing costs based on transaction specifics, and accounts for any refunds or adjustments processed during settlement periods previously.** Settlement reports SHALL clearly categorize all deduction types providing sellers with transparent accounting of net earnings calculations while supporting reconciliation with individual transaction records available through detailed transaction history systems. THE system SHALL generate settlement reports on scheduled cycles that may vary based on seller preferences and business agreement terms while supporting different settlement frequencies daily for high-volume sellers weekly for standard merchants or monthly based on individual business needs and operational processing capabilities.  

### Platform Commission and Fee Management

**THE commission calculation system SHALL automatically deduct platform fees from each transaction according to predetermined rates agreed upon with individual sellers while supporting variable commission structures based on product categories, time periods, promotional campaigns, volume thresholds, and seller performance ratings proportionate to business value generation.** Commission calculations SHALL occur automatically during payment processing ensuring accuracy and transparency while preventing manual processing errors and maintaining audit trail requirements for regulatory compliance. WHERE promotional commission rates are applied for marketing campaigns or seller acquisition programs, THE system SHALL track promotion periods accurately ensuring correct rate expiration and transition to standard commission structures at predetermined times. IF commission disputes arise due to calculation errors or business agreement disagreements, THEN THE system SHALL provide detailed transaction documentation enabling accurate reconciliation while maintaining appropriate administrative controls for rate corrections when legally permitted and properly authorized claims.  

**THE system SHALL maintain escrow account functionality for high-value transactions where seller funds remain temporarily held until delivery confirmation is received from customers or appropriate delivery verification periods expire ensuring consumer protection while maintaining seller cash flow requirements through transparent holding period communications and appropriate administrative controls over fund release processes.** Escrow periods SHALL be determined based on shipment tracking data and delivery confirmation from integrated logistics partners while supporting predetermined holding periods for digital products or services without physical delivery confirmation available. THE escrow management system SHALL track interest accruals appropriately during holding periods where required while processing automatic entitlement fees addressing platform operational costs with transparent accounting for all participating parties involved in transaction processing flows.  

## Error Handling and Exception Management

### Payment Failure Processing  

**IF payment authorization fails due to insufficient funds, THEN THE system SHALL immediately notify customers about payment failure while providing alternative payment method selection, payment amount adjustment guidance for applicable partial payment scenarios, and detailed error explanations helping customers understand resolution requirements for successful transaction processing attempts.** The system SHALL support automatic retry mechanisms for transient payment processing failures while maintaining appropriate limits preventing excessive retry attempts that could threaten payment processing security or result in payment gateway service penalties. Payment failure notifications SHALL include specific error codes meaningful to customer service representatives while providing user-friendly explanations appropriate for customer self-service resolution without requiring direct support intervention in many cases effectively reducing customer service workload while maintaining satisfaction levels through proactive problem-solving approaches.  

**THE system SHALL maintain comprehensive payment error logging systems recording all authorization failures including underlying error causes communicated by payment processors, correlation ID numbers enabling support team investigation, customer communication attempts made through the process, and successful resolution methodologies employed during customer service intervention processes.** LOG analysis SHALL identify systematic payment issues including gateway-level connectivity problems, specific card issuer authorization difficulties, customer behavioral patterns contributing to payment failures, and technical system configurations affecting payment processing success rates allowing operations teams to implement corrective measures efficiently. Payment error reports SHALL provide business stakeholders with actionable system improvement recommendations addressing authentication procedures, user interface design elements, communication clarity standards, and payment process optimization opportunities directly impacting overall customer satisfaction levels and conversion rate generation.  

### Technical Recovery and Contingency Procedures

**WHERE payment processing systems experience technical failures including payment gateway connectivity issues, database accessibility problems, network connectivity disruptions, or server processing capacity limitations, THE system SHALL implement predefined contingency procedures enabling continued transaction processing while maintaining appropriate security standards and transaction accuracy requirements.** Contingency payment processing SHALL include alternative payment gateway routing for specific payment method failures while maintaining primary gateway relationships for standard processing when primary systems return to operational status. THE system SHALL maintain offline payment processing capabilities through secure manual procedures implemented by qualified administrators for high-value transactions requiring immediate processing despite technical system unavailability including proper documentation creation, approval process workflows, and comprehensive audit trail maintenance for regulatory compliance purposes.  

## Regulatory Compliance and Legal Framework

### Financial Services Compliance

**THE payment processing system SHALL maintain strict adherence to applicable financial services regulations including anti-money laundering requiring customer identification procedures above specified transaction thresholds, consumer protection regulations ensuring fair treatment throughout payment processing activities, and anti-fraud requirements implementing comprehensive security measures preventing payment processing abuse.** THE system SHALL implement know your customer requirements through identity verification procedures for sellers processing transactions above regulatory thresholds while maintaining appropriate customer privacy protections throughout identity verification activities. WHERE seller accounts process international transactions involving potentially higher-risk jurisdictions, THE system SHALL conduct additional due diligence procedures including document verification,, enhanced monitoring, and appropriate record-keeping requirements for regulatory examination preparation.  

**THE regulatory compliance framework SHALL support comprehensive audit trail requirements maintaining detailed transaction histories including initiation timestamps, authorization confirmations, fund capture records, refund processing documentation, and chargeback dispute resolution outcomes ensuring complete regulatory compliance examination readiness.** ALL audits trail elements SHALL include permanent transaction identifiers enabling accurate cross-referencing with bank processing records gateway communications, and customer service intervention histories demonstrating transaction legitimacy and processing accuracy throughout payment lifecycle management. THE system SHALL maintain seven-year minimum retention policy for payment transaction records while implementing appropriate access controls ensuring only authorized personnel can examine sensitive payment data and supporting documentation requiring legitimate purposes including regulatory examinations, dispute resolution activities, or criminal investigation cooperation mandates providing essential infrastructure supporting legal compliance requirements.  

This comprehensive business specification ensures the ecommerceMall platform provides secure, efficient, and legally compliant payment processing capabilities supporting all stakeholder requirements while maintaining industry-leading performance standards and regulatory compliance throughout payment operation lifecycle management.
