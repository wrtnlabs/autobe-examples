# Customer Experience Requirements

## Executive Summary

This document defines the comprehensive customer experience for the shopping-mall multi-vendor e-commerce platform, focusing on creating a seamless, engaging, and personalized shopping journey. The requirements encompass all customer touchpoints from initial product discovery through post-purchase support, ensuring satisfaction and building long-term customer relationships across multiple sellers in the marketplace ecosystem.

## Customer Journey Mapping

### Pre-Purchase Experience Excellence

THE shopping-mall platform SHALL provide a frictionless browsing experience optimized for all user types including registered customers, guest users, and mobile app users. WHEN a guest visits the platform, THE system SHALL allow complete product browsing capabilities including category navigation, product search functionality, and detailed product views with seller comparisons. THE guest experience SHALL include full cart management, product wishlist creation, and comprehensive product information display including multiple images, variant details, seller information, and real-time shipping estimates.

WHILE browsing as a registered customer, THE system SHALL provide sophisticated personalization through machine learning algorithms that analyze browsing history, purchase patterns, and behavioral indicators. THESE recommendations SHALL appear dynamically across the homepage, category pages, and product detail pages, updating continuously based on real-time user behavior. THE recommendation engine SHALL consider factors including recently viewed products from any seller, frequently purchased together items across the marketplace, trending products within relevant categories, historical price sensitivity patterns, and seasonal shopping behaviors specific to the customer's geographic location.

THE product discovery experience SHALL leverage multiple intelligent pathways to help customers find desired items efficiently including visual search capabilities where customers upload images to find similar products, voice search functionality optimized for mobile devices, predictive search with autocomplete that suggests products and categories as customers type, and personalized category recommendations based on previous shopping behavior. WHEN customers search for products, THE system SHALL return comprehensive results including products from all marketplace sellers carrying matching items with integrated comparison features.

### Account Registration and Profile Management

THE account creation process SHALL require minimal information while ensuring security including email address validation, secure password setup with complexity requirements, and optional social media account integration supporting Google, Facebook, and Apple ID authentication. THE system SHALL implement progressive profiling where additional information is collected over time in exchange for personalized benefits rather than requiring everything upfront.

WHERE customers provide profile information, THE system SHALL use this data to immediately personalize their experience including preferred language localization based on geographic location, currency display for international customers, communication channel preferences for marketing and transactional messages, and delivery preferences including alternative delivery addresses and delivery timing preferences. THE profile management SHALL support multiple shipping addresses with intelligent default selection based on product categories and delivery zones.

THE customer account dashboard SHALL provide comprehensive account management including order history across all marketplace sellers with unified tracking, wishlist management with sharing capabilities, saved shopping cart items that persist across sessions, payment method management with secure storage and multiple options, communication preferences for different notification types, and privacy controls enabling customers to manage data sharing and marketing preferences.

## Review and Rating System

### Verified Purchase Review Framework

THE review system SHALL ensure authenticity by requiring purchase verification before allowing product reviews. THE system SHALL automatically verify purchases by cross-referencing customer order histories with product SKUs, eliminating manual verification delays while maintaining comprehensive fraud prevention. THE verification process SHALL exclude returns, cancelled orders, and refunds from review eligibility while maintaining transparency about verification criteria.

WHERE customers attempt to submit reviews without verified purchases, THE system SHALL provide clear explanations of verification requirements and suggest that they contact customer service if they believe this determination is incorrect. THE verification SHALL consider bundle purchases, gift purchases, and partial returns ensuring fair access to review functionality while maintaining system integrity against artificial review manipulation.

### Comprehensive Review Collection Process

THE review submission interface SHALL be accessible through multiple touchpoints including order confirmation emails sent 7 days after delivery, customer account dashboard with prominent "Rate Your Purchase" sections, and direct links within shipping confirmation emails with clear "Leave a Review" calls-to-action. THE review system SHALL support multiple media types including text reviews with 50-5000 character limits per customer segmentation, photo uploads showing actual product conditions with automatic moderation, and video reviews up to 3 minutes in length with quality guidelines.

THE review collection SHALL use intelligent timing algorithms that optimize for completion rates while ensuring genuine product experience including automatic review requests sent 7-10 days after delivery confirmation for standard products, immediate review requests for digital products upon delivery, extended review windows for seasonal products reflecting actual usage periods, and targeted follow-up communications for customers who have not reviewed products despite verified purchases.

### Review Moderation and Quality Assurance

THE review moderation system SHALL balance automation efficiency with human oversight to maintain quality and authenticity. THE automated filtering SHALL detect and flag reviews containing inappropriate language, competitor mentions, personal information exposure, external website links, advertising content, or off-topic discussions for human review within 4 business hours. THE machine learning algorithms SHALL continuously improve the detection of fake reviews through pattern analysis of reviewer behavior and linguistic characteristics.

THE review approval process SHALL maintain consistent publication timing with reviews appearing on product pages within 5 minutes of submission while ensuring appropriate content screening. THE system SHALL provide review voting mechanisms where customers can vote on review helpfulness, which influences review ranking and visibility. THE moderation system SHALL maintain reviewer anonymity while providing sellers access to reviewer history patterns for quality assessment purposes.

### Rating Aggregation and Analytics

THE rating calculation system SHALL use weighted statistical methods reflecting current product experiences while respecting historical feedback. THE overall product rating SHALL display as both numerical scores and visual star representations, updating in real-time as new reviews are processed. THE weighted algorithm SHALL consider review recency, review helpfulness votes, reviewer verification history, and comparative analysis against category averages to generate balanced product rankings.

THE seller performance analytics through the dashboard shall include comprehensive rating breakdowns showing performance trends over customizable periods, comparative analysis against marketplace average for relevant categories, specific customer feedback categorization including product quality, shipping reliability, and customer service experiences, and recognition programs highlighting top-rated sellers with increased visibility and promotional opportunities.

## Customer Support Requirements

### Multi-Channel Support Infrastructure

THE customer support system shall provide comprehensive assistance through multiple accessible channels designed to resolve customer issues efficiently and support marketplace operations excellence. THE live chat support shall operate during extended business hours covering peak shopping periods across different time zones, with specialized agents trained to handle complex multi-seller order issues including split shipments, partial returns, and seller coordination requirements.

THE email support shall guarantee response within 24 hours for routine inquiries and 4 hours for urgent matters including delivery issues affecting promised timeframes or technical problems impacting order completion. THE ticket management system shall automatically categorize and route inquiries based on keywords and customer history analysis, ensuring specialized agents handle complex issues while efficient routing maintains service level agreements.

THE phone support shall be available for premium customers achieving specified account tiers through purchase history or subscription services, and for complex situations requiring personal attention including payment disputes, account security concerns, or high-value order complications. THE integrated phone support shall provide agents with complete customer account access including order histories across all marketplace sellers, enabling informed assistance without requiring customers to repeat information or transaction details.

### Self-Service Help Portal and Knowledge Management

THE self-service help portal shall address 80% of common customer inquiries through comprehensive, searchable knowledge resources including step-by-step guides with illustrations and video demonstrations, policy explanation sections clarifying marketplace policies for customers, sellers, and platform operations, technical troubleshooting guides covering common issues including account access, payment problems, and shipping delays.

THE interactive help features shall streamline customer issue resolution through an order tracking assistant that automatically detects order-related queries in support tickets and provides real-time shipping updates, invoice and receipt generation tools allowing customers to download transaction documentation, return initiation wizards that guide customers through return processes including label generation and refund estimates based on platform policies and seller-specific return windows.

THE knowledge base content shall be continuously updated based on customer feedback analysis, emerging issue identification through support ticket categorization, and user experience testing to optimize self-service success rates. THE system shall track knowledge article usage, identify content gaps through failed search queries, and prioritize content improvements based on potential impact on customer satisfaction scores.

### Escalation Management and Quality Assurance

THE escalation process shall implement clear pathways ensuring customer issues receive appropriate attention based on complexity, severity, and business impact. THE Level 1 support shall handle general inquiries, basic account assistance, and standard processes including password resets, address changes, and order status updates using established knowledge base procedures.

THE Level 2 escalation shall address complex multi-seller order issues including payment disputes, seller performance concerns, return negotiations between multiple parties, and technical problems requiring deeper investigation across system logs and transaction histories. THE Level 2 agents shall have enhanced system access including refund processing capabilities, seller communication channels, and policy interpretation authority.

THE Level 3 escalation shall occur for situations requiring management approval, legal consultation, or resolution of platform-wide issues affecting multiple customers simultaneously. THESE cases shall include payment gateway failures, coordinated seller violations, account security breaches, or regulatory compliance matters requiring specialized handling procedures and documentation.

## Wishlist Management System

### Account-Based Wishlist Architecture

THE wishlist functionality shall operate exclusively for registered and authenticated customers, providing persistent cross-device functionality while encouraging account creation through limited "Save for Later" features for guest users. THE system shall maintain unlimited wishlist capacity with intelligent organization supporting automatic categorization by product type, custom category creation reflecting personal preferences, priority tagging systems enabling urgency or interest level indication, and collaborative features allowing customers to share selected wishlists with friends or family members for gift coordination.

THE wishlist cross-seller management shall seamlessly handle products from multiple marketplace sellers while maintaining usability despite potential complexity. WHEN customers add items from different sellers to a single wishlist, THE system shall preserve seller-specific information including pricing, shipping costs, availability status, and promotional information while providing unified layout and browsing experiences. THE availability monitoring shall continuously watch wishlist items across all sellers, alerting customers when items return to stock, become available from alternative sellers, or when prices decrease below specified thresholds.

### Price and Availability Intelligence

THE price tracking functionality shall monitor wishlist items continuously across all marketplace sellers providing intelligent notification systems for price-sensitive customers. THE price drop alerts shall notify customers when items decrease by specified percentages or predetermined amounts, with customizable notification frequencies and price thresholds based on item categories or individual product preferences. THE stock level monitoring shall provide availability alerts when wishlist items become available from sellers that may offer better terms including improved shipping costs, faster delivery options, or favorable return policies.

THE wishlist analytics shall provide customers with comprehensive insights including price history graphs showing trending patterns and optimal purchase timing recommendations, availability probability estimates based on seller performance data and seasonal patterns, and recommendation engines suggesting similar products or complementary items based on wishlist contents and purchase history correlations across the marketplace ecosystem.

### Integrated Shopping Experience

THE wishlist-to-purchase workflow shall provide comprehensive purchasing options while maintaining shopping flexibility and supporting customer decision optimization. THE bulk purchase functionality shall enable customers to add multiple wishlist items to cart simultaneously with automatic grouping by seller to optimize checkout experiences and minimize shipping complications. THE save-for-later integration shall allow seamless transfer of saved items from shopping cart to wishlist for future consideration without losing product variant selections, quantity preferences, or seller specifications.

THE gift registry functionality shall extend wishlist capabilities for special occasions including weddings, birthdays, or holidays with date-specific tracking, wishlist sharing options through email or social media, and guest privacy settings allowing public or private visibility controls. THE collaborative shopping features shall enable friends or family members to suggest additions, comment on selections, or coordinate group purchases directly through platform communications while maintaining gift recipient surprise elements when necessary.

## Notification System Architecture

### Personalized Communication Channels

THE notification system shall adapt intelligently to customer preferences while maintaining essential communications about account status, order management, and security events. THE email communications shall serve as the primary channel for important updates including order confirmations, shipping notifications, account security alerts, and regulatory policy changes or updates. THE notification management shall provide granular control enabling opt-out of promotional emails while maintaining critical account and order communications through other preferred channels.

THE mobile push notification systems shall be available for customers installing and authorizing platform applications with restrictions ensuring only immediately relevant updates including order status changes, delivery tracking notifications, and time-sensitive offers on customer wishlist items below preferred price thresholds. THE SMS notifications shall be optional and customer-controlled without service fees to customers, primarily used for delivery coordination requiring real-time communication through final delivery phases while maintaining customer privacy through automatic number masking.

### Intelligent Content Targeting

THE notification system shall leverage customer data analysis and shopping behavior patterns to provide genuinely useful communications rather than intrusive marketing messages. THE wishlist notifications shall inform customers of price drops, restocking events, or limited-time offers on saved items within customer-specified price and interest thresholds while preventing notification spam through intelligent grouping and scheduling algorithms. THE abandoned cart reminders shall gently encourage checkout completion through helpful reminders including estimated shipping timeframes, inventory availability warnings, and relevant seller communications about item availability.

THE personalized recommendation notifications shall consider customer purchase history across sellers, browsing patterns through multiple sessions, and stated preferences to suggest relevant products from multiple marketplace sellers or complementary accessories and services. THE category trend notifications shall alert interested customers to new marketplace sellers, trending products in preferred categories, and seasonal items aligned with demonstrated shopping interests through historical order and wishlist activity across the platform ecosystem.

### Advanced Notification Management

THE notification preferences shall provide customers granular control over communication types and delivery frequency through comprehensive settings enabling precise communication channel management by message category. THE frequency management shall include daily, weekly, or monthly digest formats for non-urgent communications, reducing notification fatigue while maintaining customer awareness of platform updates and personal account changes. THE time zone awareness shall schedule bulk communications during appropriate local hours while considering customer-specified communication preferences for promotional versus account-related messages.

THE notification system shall implement intelligent delivery timing algorithms considering customer time zones, historical communication engagement patterns, and optimal open rates for different message types based on customer segmentation analysis. THE cross-platform synchronization shall ensure consistent notification states across mobile applications, web interfaces, and email communications avoiding duplicate messages while maintaining message acknowledgment tracking across all communication channels.

## Personalization and Customer Intelligence

### Cognitive Shopping Experience System

THE platform shall provide increasingly personalized experiences by learning from customer behavior patterns and explicitly stated preferences through profile management and feature usage analysis. THE browsing personalization shall adapt product discovery based on viewed items, search terms used across sessions, sellers followed through bookmark systems, categories frequently browsed, and seasonal timing patterns for gift-giving or personal shopping behaviors.

THE recommendation engine shall develop sophisticated cross-category intelligence where fashion customers receive tailored accessory suggestions, electronics customers see relevant component or accessory recommendations, and home improvement browsers receive complementary tools or material suggestions from different marketplace sellers based on predictive purchase patterns. THE behavioral analysis shall combine historical data with real-time browsing signals to maintain current relevance while respecting privacy preferences and usage limitations.

### Dynamic Interface Personalization

THE platform interface shall adapt intelligently to individual customer preferences while maintaining usability standards and accessibility requirements including screen reader compatibility and keyboard navigation support. THE layout preferences shall allow customers to choose between visual layouts optimized for different device types, control product displays per page based on browsing speed and device capabilities, select preferred sorting methods for search results and category browsing, and access alternative navigation methods for improved accessibility when required.

THE content personalization shall modify homepage displays, featured product sections, and promotional areas based on customer profiles while maintaining discovery opportunities for new marketplace sellers and product categories outside established preferences. THE new seller discovery shall balance algorithmic preferences with random or trend-based introductions ensuring customers explore marketplace diversity while maintaining relevance to established shopping patterns and demonstrated interests across product categories and seller types.

### Advanced Analytics and Insights

THE customer intelligence system shall accumulate insights over time while maintaining strict privacy standards and providing customer controls for data usage and retention preferences. THE activity tracking shall focus on recent behaviors that inform immediate personalization without creating permanent behavioral profiles allowing customers to explore new interests without impacting established preferences through temporary browsing or research activities. THE purchase history analysis shall identify patterns enabling predictive features including subscription renewal reminders for consumable products from recurring sellers, seasonal timing recognition for holiday or gift purchase patterns, and replacement cycle awareness for durable goods requiring eventual replacement or upgrade.

THE demographic intelligence shall refine personalization accuracy when voluntarily provided through profile completion, survey participation, or stated preferences while maintaining strict data protection controls and opt-out capabilities for privacy-conscious customers. THE data retention policies shall automatically purge behavioral tracking data according to established schedules while preserving essential account information and purchase histories necessary for customer service and account management functions.

## Service Recovery and Customer Satisfaction

### Proactive Issue Identification and Response

THE service recovery system shall continuously monitor platform operations to identify potential service issues before they significantly impact customer satisfaction including delivery monitoring across all marketplace sellers and shipping carriers, payment processing success rates and authorization failures across payment methods, system performance metrics and response time degradation, seller performance indicators including customer satisfaction scores and policy compliance rates, and customer support ticket trends indicating emerging issues.

THE proactive customer communication shall alert affected customers immediately when service issues are detected that may impact their orders or account access including delayed deliveries beyond advertised timeframes with updated estimates and compensation options, payment processing failures with alternative method suggestions and retry opportunities, inventory discrepancies where ordered products become unavailable with substitution or cancellation alternatives, seller performance issues requiring intervention or policy enforcement, and platform-wide technical problems requiring customer action or affecting transaction processing.

### Swift Resolution and Customer Recovery

THE customer recovery process shall focus on satisfaction restoration over operational convenience through immediate acknowledgment channels, personal communication options for complex situations, comprehensive documentation and progress tracking systems, and flexible resolution options tailored to individual customer preferences and situation severity. THE resolution options shall include immediate account credits for service failures, expedited processing or shipping upgrades at platform expense, complimentary services or products as appropriate compensation for frustration or inconvenience, flexible return options and full refund processing where standard policies may not apply, and priority customer service access with senior agent assignment.

THE follow-up communication shall ensure customer satisfaction with problem resolution quality and offer additional assistance when needed while gathering feedback to inform platform improvement initiatives through satisfaction surveys, trend analysis of service recovery cases, identification of systemic issues requiring policy changes, and development of preventive measures based on root cause analysis. THE customer relationship building through effective service recovery shall transform problem situations into opportunities to demonstrate exceptional commitment to customer satisfaction and platform reliability that exceeds customer expectations and industry standards.
