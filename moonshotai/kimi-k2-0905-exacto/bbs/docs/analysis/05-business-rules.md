# Business Rules and Operational Policy

## Introduction and Purpose

This document defines the comprehensive business rules, validation logic, and operational policies for the politicsBbs discussion board system. These rules govern how the system behaves in real-world scenarios, ensuring consistent operation while maintaining simplicity and focusing on economic and political discourse. The business rules serve as the bridge between user requirements and technical implementation, providing clear guidelines for content management, user behavior, and system operation.

WHEN implementing the politicsBbs system, THE development team SHALL enforce all business rules specified in this document to maintain platform integrity and user safety. THE system SHALL provide clear feedback to users when business rules are violated, helping them understand and comply with community standards.

## Content Rules (Economic/Political Focus)

### Core Content Requirements

THE politicsBbs system SHALL require all content to maintain focus on economic and political discussions. WHEN users create new articles, THE system SHALL enforce that content addresses economic policies, political analysis, current economic conditions, or political developments at local, national, or international levels.

WHEN content is submitted, THE system SHALL automatically validate that it contains relevant keywords or topics related to economics or politics. IF content appears unrelated to economic or political topics, THEN THE system SHALL reject the submission with a clear message explaining that discussions must relate to economic or political matters.

THE system SHALL allow discussions on topics including but not limited to: economic policy debates, political current events, international trade discussions, taxation policy analysis, government spending evaluation, election coverage and analysis, regulatory policy impacts, market regulation debates, social policy economic implications, and geopolitical economic relationships.

### Content Quality Standards

THE system SHALL enforce minimum content quality standards. WHEN users create articles, THE system SHALL require at least 50 words of substantive content. IF submitted content contains fewer than 50 words, THEN THE system SHALL reject the submission and request more substantial discussion frames.

WHILE creating content, THE system SHALL provide real-time preview showing how the finished article will appear to readers. WHEN users click submit, THE system SHALL validate content formatting, ensuring that special characters display correctly and that any embedded links are properly formatted.

WHERE articles include statistical or factual claims, THE system SHALL encourage users to cite sources but SHALL NOT require mandatory source attribution. THE system SHALL provide optional reference fields for users who wish to include supporting documentation or source links.

### Content Restrictions and Prohibitions

THE politicsBbs system SHALL strictly prohibit certain content types regardless of their economic or political relevance. WHEN content is submitted, THE system SHALL automatically scan and reject any material containing personal attacks, hate speech, threats of violence, or discriminatory language based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, or national origin.

WHEN users submit content, THE system SHALL evaluate text for potential personal attacks directed at individuals rather than their policies or arguments. IF content contains direct personal insults, name-calling, or character attacks on individuals or groups, THEN THE system SHALL automatically reject the content and request revision to maintain focus on issues rather than personalities.

THE system SHALL allow vigorous policy debate while prohibiting inflammatory rhetoric that could lead to real-world conflict. WHEN controversial political topics are discussed, THE system SHALL encourage evidence-based arguments while preventing hate speech or incitement to violence.
