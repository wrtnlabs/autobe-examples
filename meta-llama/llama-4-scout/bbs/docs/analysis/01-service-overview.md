# Service Overview Document

The purpose of this document is to provide a high-level overview of the simple economic/political discussion board service.

## Purpose

The discussion board is designed to facilitate economic and political discussions among users. It allows users to create articles, comment on existing articles, and attach images and files to their posts.

## Scope

The scope of this service includes:
- User registration and authentication
- Article creation, editing, and deletion
- Comment creation, editing, and deletion
- Image and file attachment
- Basic search functionality

## Target Audience

The target audience for this discussion board includes:
- Economists and political analysts
- Students and researchers
- General users interested in economic and political discussions

## Functional Requirements

### User Actors

The system shall support the following user actors:
- Guest: Can view public articles and discussions
- Member: Can create, edit, and delete their own articles and comments
- Admin: Can manage all articles, comments, and user accounts

### Article Requirements

- Articles shall have a title and content
- Articles shall support image and file attachments
- Articles shall be searchable by keyword

### Comment Requirements

- Comments shall have text content
- Comments shall be associated with a specific article
- Comments shall support replies

### Attachment Requirements

- Images and files shall be attachable to articles and comments
- The system shall support common image and file formats

## Non-Functional Requirements

### Security

- The system shall implement user authentication and authorization
- The system shall ensure data encryption for sensitive information

### Performance

- The system shall respond to user interactions within 2 seconds
- The system shall handle a minimum of 100 concurrent users

## Business Requirements

### Business Model

The discussion board shall operate as a free, open platform for economic and political discussions.

### Revenue Strategy

The platform shall generate revenue through optional paid features for premium users.
