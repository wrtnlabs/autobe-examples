# Service Overview

## Introduction

This document outlines the concept for a straightforward and user-friendly online discussion board focused on economic and political topics. The primary goal is to create a minimal, yet effective, platform that encourages thoughtful conversation and debate. Users will be able to share their perspectives by creating articles, which can be enhanced with image and file attachments, and engage with others through comments. The design philosophy emphasizes simplicity and core functionality, avoiding unnecessary complexity to ensure the platform is intuitive and accessible to everyone.

## Vision and Mission

**Vision**: To be a premier online destination for civil and substantive discussion on economics and politics, where diverse viewpoints can be shared and respectfully debated.

**Mission**: To provide a simple, secure, and accessible platform that empowers individuals to engage in meaningful dialogue. We aim to foster an informed community by enabling users to create, share, and discuss content, supported by essential features like multimedia attachments and clear moderation.

## Core Features

The discussion board will be built around a focused set of essential features:

*   **Article Management**: Authenticated members can create, edit, and delete their own articles. This forms the basis of content creation on the platform.
*   **Commenting System**: Members can post comments on articles, allowing for direct engagement and discussion.
*   **File & Image Attachments**: To support detailed and evidence-based discussions, members can attach images (e.g., charts, photos) and files (e.g., PDFs, documents) to their articles.
*   **User Roles & Authentication**: The system will support three distinct user roles:
    *   **Guests**: Can browse and read all public content without an account.
    *   **Members**: Can create accounts, log in, and participate fully by posting articles and comments.
    *   **Admins**: Can moderate content and manage users to ensure a healthy community environment.
*   **Content Browsing**: All content will be publicly accessible for reading, organized in a clear and easy-to-navigate format.

## Target Audience

This service is intended for a broad audience interested in economics and politics, including:

*   **Informed Citizens**: General users who follow current events and wish to participate in public discourse.
*   **Students and Academics**: Individuals from high school to university level who are studying or researching topics in politics, economics, and social sciences.
*   **Professionals and Hobbyists**: Journalists, policy analysts, economists, and enthusiasts who want a platform to share insights and debate ideas.
*   **Casual Readers**: Individuals who prefer to stay informed by reading different perspectives without actively participating in discussions.
