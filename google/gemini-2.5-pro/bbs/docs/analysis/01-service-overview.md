# Service Overview: Economic & Political Discussion Board

## 1. Introduction

This document provides a high-level business and strategic overview of the **discussionBoard** service. Its purpose is to establish the foundational vision, goals, intended audience, and core functional scope of the project. It serves as the primary guiding document for all stakeholders, including developers, designers, and project managers, to ensure that the final product aligns with the core objective: creating a simple, focused, and high-quality platform for economic and political discourse. This document intentionally omits technical specifications, focusing instead on the "what" and "why" from a business perspective.

## 2. Service Vision

To cultivate a dedicated and accessible online platform for thoughtful, evidence-based, and respectful discussions on economic and political topics. We envision a niche community where users engage in substantive conversations, share informed perspectives, and access high-quality content, standing as a clear alternative to mainstream social media by prioritizing depth of discussion over superficial engagement.

## 3. Project Goals

To realize our vision, this project will adhere to the following primary goals:

-   **Champion Simplicity**: Develop a minimalist, stable, and intuitive discussion board. We will consciously avoid feature bloat and unnecessary complexity, focusing exclusively on the essential functionalities that support quality conversation.
-   **Enable Rich Content Creation**: Equip members with the tools to create content-rich articles that include not only text but also essential image and file attachments to substantiate their arguments and analyses.
-   **Foster Focused Community Interaction**: Implement a clean and straightforward commenting system that enables direct engagement with articles, forming the backbone of community discussion without cumbersome distractions.
-   **Ensure Community Health and Safety**: Provide administrators with fundamental, effective moderation tools to manage content and users, thereby maintaining a healthy, constructive, and on-topic discussion environment.

## 4. Target Audience

The platform is specifically designed for individuals who possess a genuine and active interest in economic and political subjects. This audience values depth, nuance, and respectful debate over sensationalism. It includes:

-   **Students and Academics**: Those studying or researching economics, political science, international relations, and adjacent fields who seek a forum for serious academic discussion.
-   **Industry Professionals**: Individuals working in finance, public policy, law, journalism, and other sectors where these topics are of practical and professional importance.
-   **Informed Enthusiasts**: Anyone passionate about engaging in civil, evidence-based discourse on current events, political theory, and economic trends, who are seeking a dedicated space away from the noise of large-scale social networks.

## 5. Core Features

In line with the project's minimalist philosophy, the discussion board will be built with a focused set of core features. Detailed specifications for each are provided in their respective requirements documents.

-   **Article Management**: This is the foundation of the platform, allowing registered members to create, edit, and delete their own articles. It empowers contributors to set the agenda and introduce topics for debate. Guests have read-only access, ensuring content is open and accessible to all.
-   **Commenting System**: This feature is the primary mechanism for interaction and debate. Members can post comments on articles, creating threads of conversation. This simple yet essential feature transforms the platform from a static publication into a dynamic community space.
-   **User Authentication**: A basic and secure authentication system allows users to register as `members`, giving them an identity on the platform and the ability to contribute content. It separates passive `guests` from active contributors, which is a critical component for community building and accountability.
-   **File & Image Attachments**: To facilitate detailed, evidence-based discussions, members can attach images (e.g., charts, graphs) and documents (e.g., PDFs of research papers) to their articles. This feature directly supports the goal of high-quality, informed discourse.
-   **Administration and Moderation**: To protect the community's health, `admin` users will have the necessary privileges to moderate the platform. This includes the ability to remove content that violates guidelines and manage users, ensuring discussions remain civil and productive.

## 6. Out of Scope

To maintain focus on a minimal viable product and adhere to the core goal of simplicity, the following features are explicitly **out of scope** for the initial version of this project:

-   **Rich-Text Editor**: The article and comment editor will support basic text formatting (e.g., Markdown) but will not be a complex "What You See Is What You Get" (WYSIWYG) editor.
-   **Real-Time Notifications**: The system will not have real-time, in-app notifications (e.g., a notification bell). Communication about replies or mentions is not part of the initial build.
-   **User Profiles and Social Features**: There will be no detailed user profile pages, follower systems, user-to-user direct messaging, or other social networking features. The focus is on the content, not on individual user profiles.
-   **Advanced Search and Filtering**: The initial search functionality will be basic, supporting keyword searches on titles and content. Advanced filtering by date range, author, or tags will not be implemented.
-   **Content Monetization or Advertising**: The platform will be non-commercial, with no features for advertising, paid content, or subscriptions.
-   **Gamification**: There will be no upvoting/downvoting, reputation scores, badges, or other gamification mechanics. The value of a contribution is determined by its content alone.

## 7. Business Model

### Why This Service Exists

In a digital landscape dominated by large, algorithm-driven social media platforms, it is difficult to find a dedicated space for focused, long-form discussion on complex topics like economics and politics. This service fills that gap by providing a simple, topic-centric forum that prioritizes content quality and civil discourse over engagement metrics and advertising revenue.

### Revenue Strategy

The service will be launched as a non-commercial, community-focused project. The primary goal is to build an intellectually valuable community, not to generate profit. Therefore, there will be no advertisements, data monetization, or subscription fees. Should the community grow to a point where operating costs become a concern, a voluntary, non-intrusive donation model may be considered in the distant future. This is outside the scope of the initial development.

### Growth Plan

Initial growth will be pursued organically by targeting existing online communities, academic circles, and professional groups with a demonstrable interest in economics and politics. The strategy is to attract a core group of high-quality contributors whose content will, in turn, draw in a larger audience of readers and participants.

## 8. Success Metrics

As a non-commercial project, success will not be measured in financial terms but rather in the health and quality of the community. The key performance indicators will be:

-   **Sustained User Engagement**: A consistent, healthy number of new articles and comments posted weekly, indicating an active and lively community.
-   **Organic Community Growth**: A steady, manageable increase in new member registrations month-over-month, demonstrating the platform's appeal to its target audience.
-   **Depth of Discussion**: A healthy ratio of comments per article, suggesting that the content is substantive and capable of stimulating meaningful conversation.
-   **User Retention**: A high percentage of members who return to the platform on a monthly basis (Monthly Active Users), proving that the service provides ongoing value.
-   **Quality of Discourse (Qualitative)**: Regular review of on-platform discussions to ensure they remain aligned with the vision of a civil, respectful, and evidence-based community.
