# Business Goals

This document outlines the strategic business objectives for the discussion board project. Its purpose is to define what success looks like and how it will be measured, ensuring that development work is consistently aligned with these foundational aims.

## Primary Objective

THE primary objective of this service SHALL be to cultivate a vibrant, engaged, and high-quality online community dedicated to thoughtful discussion on economic and political topics. The platform aims to become a reputable and trusted space for users to share insights, post well-reasoned articles, and engage in constructive debate.

## Secondary Objectives

To support the primary objective, the following secondary goals have been established:

*   **Foster Active User Participation**: Encourage members to consistently create content and interact with others. Success is not just measured by readership, but by the volume and quality of active contributions.
*   **Ensure a High-Quality User Experience**: Provide a simple, intuitive, and straightforward interface that allows users to focus on the content and discussions without unnecessary complexity or friction, as stated in the initial project request.
*   **Achieve Sustainable Community Growth**: Steadily increase the number of registered and active members by building a reputation for high-quality discourse.
*   **Maintain Content Integrity**: Uphold a high standard for all community content through effective and fair moderation, ensuring the platform remains free of spam and low-quality, off-topic posts.

## Success Metrics (KPIs)

To measure progress towards our objectives, the following Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) will be monitored. These metrics reflect the health and success of the community from a business perspective.

| Category | KPI | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Community Activity** | **Number of New Articles per Week** | Measures the rate at which new discussions are being initiated by members. |
| | **Average Comments per Article** | Indicates the level of engagement and interaction each article generates. |
| | **Active Member Ratio** | The percentage of registered members who create at least one post or comment within a 30-day period. This reflects the core active community size. |
| **Community Growth** | **Monthly Active Users (MAU)** | The total number of unique users (guests and members) who visit the site at least once per month. |
| | **New Member Registrations per Month** | Tracks the rate at which the community is attracting new members. |
| | **Member Retention Rate** | The percentage of members who return to the platform in the subsequent month. This is a key indicator of user satisfaction and value. |
| **User Experience** | **Time per Session** | The average amount of time a user spends on the site during a single visit. Longer session times suggest deeper engagement. |
| **Content Quality** | **Content Moderation Rate** | The percentage of posts or comments that require administrative intervention (e.g., editing or removal). A lower rate is indicative of higher overall content quality from the community. |
