registration. Guests represent the general public accessing economic and
political discussion content. This table tracks guest-level interactions
and access patterns for platform analytics, though guests have no
personal information or managed entities.

### `economic_discussion_board_articles`

Core content unit where members publish articles on economic and
political topics. Each article serves as the foundation for discussion,
containing title and body text that can be enhanced with various
attachments for supporting evidence and data.

Properties as follows:

- `id`: Primary Key.
- `economic_discussion_board_member_id`
  > Authenticated member who created this article. {@link
  > economic_discussion_board_members.id}
- `title`: Article title limited to 200 characters for concise topic identification.
- `body`
  > Main article content supporting rich text formatting and up to 10,000
  > characters for comprehensive economic analysis.
- `status`
  > Publication status indicating article state: draft, pending (awaiting
  > moderation), published (visible to all), rejected (moderation denied).
- `created_at`: Timestamp when the article was first created.
- `updated_at`: Timestamp when the article was last modified.
- `deleted_at`: Soft delete timestamp for removed articles.

### `economic_discussion_board_attachments`

Supporting materials attached to articles, including images and documents

Each attachment belongs to exactly one article.

Properties as follows:

- `id`: Primary Key.
- `economic_discussion_board_article_id`

  > economic_discussion_board_articles.id}
- `name`: Original filename of the uploaded attachment.
- `path`: Storage path or URL where the attachment file is located.
- `type`: File type of the attachment (e.g., 'image/jpeg', 'application/pdf').
- `size`: File size in bytes for storage management.
- `created_at`: Timestamp when the attachment was uploaded.
- `updated_at`: Timestamp when the attachment was last modified.

### `economic_discussion_board_comments`

User comments on published articles enabling threaded discussions and
community engagement. Each comment belongs to a specific article and is
created by an authenticated member through their active session. Comments
can have parent references to create nested reply threads up to 3 levels
deep. Members can edit their own comments after posting, and
administrators can moderate inappropriate content through soft deletion.
Comments appear immediately upon submission but may be moderated by
admins, maintaining chronological ordering within discussion threads.

Properties as follows:

- `id`: Primary Key.
- `economic_discussion_board_article_id`: Belonged article's [economic_discussion_board_articles.id](#economic_discussion_board_articles).
- `economic_discussion_board_member_id`: Commented member's [economic_discussion_board_members.id](#economic_discussion_board_members).
- `economic_discussion_board_member_session_id`
  > Belonged member's authentication session's {@link
  > economic_discussion_board_member_sessions.id}.
- `parent_id`
  > Parent comment's [economic_discussion_board_comments.id](#economic_discussion_board_comments) for
  > threaded replies.
- `body`
  > Comment text content with maximum length of 2000 characters for
  > meaningful discussion contributions.
- `created_at`: Timestamp when the comment was first created and published.
- `updated_at`: Timestamp when the comment was last modified by the author.
- `deleted_at`
  > Soft delete timestamp for moderated content removal while preserving
  > audit trails.
