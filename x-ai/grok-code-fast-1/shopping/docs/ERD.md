### `shopping_mall_carts`

Shopping cart entity representing a customer's active shopping session.

Stores the state of a customer's shopping activities, enabling persistent
cart functionality across browser sessions and device changes. Each cart
belongs to exactly one customer and contains items selected for potential
purchase.

Carts are created implicitly when a customer adds their first item and
persist for 30 days even when logged out. They serve as the container for
shopping cart items and transition into orders during checkout.

The cart lifecycle includes creation through first item addition, updates
through item modifications, and either conversion to orders or automatic
cleanup after inactivity periods.

### `shopping_mall_coupons`

Coupon definitions and configurations for promotional discount campaigns.
Supports various discount types including percentage discounts, fixed
amount reductions, and special offer promotions. Each coupon has unique
code for customer redemption, configurable usage limits, and time-based
validity windows. Coupons can be targeted at specific customer segments
or applied platform-wide.

Properties as follows:

- `id`: Primary Key.
- `code`
  > Unique coupon code for customer redemption. Case-insensitive but stored
  > in uppercase for consistency.
- `type`
  > Discount calculation method: 'percentage' for percent-off, 'fixed' for
  > dollar amount reduction, 'free_shipping' for shipping waivers.
- `value`
  > Discount amount or percentage value depending on type field. For
  > percentage type, represents percent (e.g., 15.0 for 15% off).
- `minimum_order_amount`
  > Minimum cart subtotal required for coupon application. Prevents misuse on
  > small orders.
- `usage_limit`
  > Maximum number of redemptions allowed across all customers. Null
  > indicates unlimited usage.
- `usage_limit_per_customer`
  > Maximum redemptions per individual customer. Prevents single customer
  > abuse.
- `expires_at`
  > Coupon expiration date and time. Coupons become unusable after this
  > timestamp.
- `is_active`
  > Whether the coupon is currently available for redemption. Allows for
  > temporary deactivation without deletion.
- `created_at`: Coupon creation timestamp for audit trails.
- `updated_at`: Last modification timestamp for tracking coupon updates.

### `shopping_mall_coupon_redemptions`

Records of individual coupon usage instances. Tracks which coupon was
applied to which cart or order, when it was redeemed, and by which
customer. Supports audit trails for discount applications and prevents
double-redemption through unique constraints.

Properties as follows:

- `id`: Primary Key.
- `shopping_mall_coupon_id`: Reference to the applied coupon. [shopping_mall_coupons.id](#shopping_mall_coupons)
- `shopping_mall_customer_id`: Customer who redeemed the coupon. [shopping_mall_customers.id](#shopping_mall_customers)
- `shopping_mall_cart_id`
  > Cart where coupon was applied (if redemption occurred during checkout).
  > [shopping_mall_carts.id](#shopping_mall_carts)
- `shopping_mall_order_id`
  > Order where coupon was used (populated when order is placed). {@link
  > shopping_mall_orders.id}
- `discounted_amount`
  > Actual discount amount applied to the cart/order. Calculated based on
  > coupon type and rules.
- `redeemed_at`: Timestamp when the coupon was redeemed by the customer.

### `shopping_mall_wishlists`

Customer wishlists for saving desired products for future purchase
consideration.

This entity manages user-created lists where customers can collect and
organize products they are interested in buying later. Each wishlist
belongs to exactly one customer and can contain multiple product items.
Wishlists enhance the shopping experience by allowing customers to track
items across browsing sessions and receive notifications when items
become restocked or go on sale.

Wishlists support multiple lists per customer for organizational
purposes, such as gift lists, personal purchases, or saving items for
different shopping occasions. The system maintains audit trails for
wishlist creation and modifications to support user account management
and historical tracking.

Properties as follows:

- `id`: Primary Key.
- `shopping_mall_customer_id`
  > Belonged customer's [shopping_mall_customers.id](#shopping_mall_customers)
  >
  > Foreign key linking the wishlist to the customer who created and owns it.
- `name`: Wishlist name chosen by the customer for easy identification.
- `description`: Optional description of the wishlist's purpose or contents.
- `created_at`: Timestamp when the wishlist was created.
- `updated_at`: Timestamp of the last modification to the wishlist.
- `deleted_at`: Soft delete timestamp for audit trail and potential recovery.

### `shopping_mall_wishlist_items`

Individual product items within customer wishlists.

This entity connects specific product variants to wishlists, allowing
customers to save particular product configurations. Each wishlist item
represents one product variant that a customer has added to their
wishlist. Items include optional quantity information for customers who
are saving multiple units of the same product.

Wishlist items enable customers to track product availability changes,
receive restock notifications, and move items to their shopping cart when
ready to purchase. The system prevents duplication of the same product
variant within a single wishlist.

Properties as follows:

- `id`: Primary Key.
- `shopping_mall_wishlist_id`
  > Belonged wishlist's [shopping_mall_wishlists.id](#shopping_mall_wishlists)
  >
  > Foreign key linking the item to its parent wishlist.
- `shopping_mall_product_variant_id`
  > Referenced product variant's [shopping_mall_product_variants.id](#shopping_mall_product_variants)
  >
  > Foreign key to the specific product variant being saved in the wishlist.
- `quantity`: Number of units the customer wants to save for this product variant.
- `created_at`: Timestamp when the item was added to the wishlist.

### `shopping_mall_inquiries`

Customer inquiries submitted through the platform for product information
or support. Inquiries enable customers to ask questions about products,
sellers, or platform services, supporting the marketplace's communication
needs. Each inquiry is created by a logged-in customer and may be
associated with specific sellers or products. Inquiries remain accessible
even after responses are provided, maintaining a complete conversation
history.

Properties as follows:

- `id`: Primary Key.
- `shopping_mall_customer_id`: Customer who submitted the inquiry. [shopping_mall_customers.id](#shopping_mall_customers)
- `shopping_mall_customer_session_id`
  > Customer's active session when submitting the inquiry. {@link
  > shopping_mall_customer_sessions.id}
- `title`
  > Short descriptive title for the inquiry. Provides quick context for the
  > question.
- `body`
  > Detailed content of the customer's inquiry. Contains the full question
  > and any supporting details.
- `secret`
  > Whether this inquiry should be kept private between customer and seller.
  > Private inquiries are not visible to other users.
- `created_at`: Timestamp when the inquiry was first submitted.
- `updated_at`: Timestamp when the inquiry was last modified.

### `shopping_mall_inquiry_responses`

Seller responses to customer inquiries submitted on the platform. Each
response addresses a specific inquiry, providing seller support and
information to customers. Responses are created by sellers and linked to
the corresponding inquiry, maintaining conversation threads. Multiple
responses per inquiry are supported for comprehensive support
interactions.

Properties as follows:

- `id`: Primary Key.
- `shopping_mall_inquiry_id`
  > The inquiry that this response addresses. {@link
  > shopping_mall_inquiries.id}
- `shopping_mall_seller_id`
  > Seller providing the response to the inquiry. {@link
  > shopping_mall_sellers.id}
- `shopping_mall_seller_session_id`
  > Seller's active session when submitting the response. {@link
  > shopping_mall_seller_sessions.id}
- `body`
  > Detailed content of the seller's response. Provides comprehensive answer
  > to the customer's inquiry.
- `created_at`: Timestamp when the response was first submitted.
- `updated_at`: Timestamp when the response was last modified.
