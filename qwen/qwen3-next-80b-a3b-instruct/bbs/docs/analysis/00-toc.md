# Discussion Board Service Overview

## Service Vision

This is a simple, minimal platform for open economic and political discussion. It exists because people need a public, accessible space to share ideas about how economies work and how political systems affect their lives — without censorship, algorithmic manipulation, or corporate mediation. Unlike mainstream social media platforms that prioritize engagement over dialogue, this board is designed for thoughtful exchange. It is not a feed. It is not a popularity contest. It is a place for discussion.

The service was conceived to fill a gap: existing forums are either overly complex, cluttered with ads, require registration just to read, or are dominated by toxicity and moderation bias. This system offers clean, readable threads with no artificial engagement metrics. No likes. No shares. No trending topics. Just posts, comments, and the freedom to speak.

## Core Value Proposition

This platform provides:

- **Ownership of thought**: Users control their ideas. Content is persistent, searchable, and not buried by algorithms.
- **Fragile openness**: No gatekeeping. Registration is free, simple, and requires only an email — no phone number, no social media link.
- **Minimalist design**: The interface focuses only on writing, reading, and replying. No distractions.
- **Attachment support**: Users may attach images and files to substantiate claims — charts, graphs, documents, or screenshots — enabling evidence-based discussion.
- **Light moderation**: Only two actor roles exist: Citizen and Moderator. Moderators act only when rules are broken, not to shape opinion.

Unlike social networks that profit by keeping users engaged, this platform profits nothing. Its value is in its existence as a neutral space.

## Target Audience

### Primary Users: Citizens

- People interested in economic policy, taxation, banking, labor rights, trade, inflation, and monetary systems.
- Individuals engaged in political discourse: elections, governance, civil liberties, international relations, and public accountability.
- Students, researchers, journalists, activists, and everyday citizens who wish to contribute to public understanding.
- People who distrust mainstream platforms for their bias, censorship, or manipulation.

### Secondary Users: Moderators

- Volunteer community members chosen by existing users (or appointed by service owner).
- Responsible only for enforcing basic rules — no opinion control.
- Trained to apply rules consistently and transparently.

## Why This Service Exists

Modern public discourse has become broken. Economic and political arguments are drowned out by noise, outrage, and misinformation. Mainstream platforms encourage polarization by rewarding extremes. These systems do not foster understanding — they monetize division.

This service exists to offer an alternative: a quiet, readable, trustworthy environment where ideas are judged by merit, not virality. It does not grow by hooking users. It grows by offering value to those who truly seek understanding.

It is not major. It is not viral. It is not for everyone. But for those who want to think — not just react — this is for them.

## How It Works (Conceptually)

A citizen:
- Registers with an email.
- Writes a post on an economic or political topic.
- Attaches a chart, PDF, or image to support their point.
- Publishes it.
- Others read, comment, or refer to it.
- Moderators remove content only if it violates the three core rules:
  1. No direct threats of violence.
  2. No doxxing (publishing private personal information).
  3. No repeated spam or bot-like behavior.

No other moderation exists. No downvotes. No replies-only restrictions. No shadowbanning.

## Growth Plan

- No marketing budget.
- Growth is organic: through word-of-mouth among economists, teachers, civic groups, and researchers.
- Linking to posts from academic papers, newsletters, or podcasts will drive traffic.
- No paid ads. No affiliate links. No sponsorships.

The value is in its integrity. Its reputation is its only currency.

## Success Metrics

- **Daily active users (DAU)**: 100–500 engaged participants
- **Post-to-comment ratio**: Above 1:2 (meaning most posts spark discussion)
- **Average thread length**: 5+ comments per thread
- **Attachment usage rate**: At least 30% of posts include images or files
- **Moderation rate**: Less than 2 removals per week
- **User retention**: 60% return within 30 days

If these metrics are met, the service has succeeded in its mission: fostering thoughtful, evidence-based conversation.
