# Product Reviews and Ratings System

## Review Submission Requirements

WHEN a customer purchases a product, THE system SHALL allow them to submit a review after 24 hours from confirmed delivery.

WHEN a customer attempts to submit a review for a product they have not purchased, THE system SHALL deny the submission and display: "You must have purchased this product to leave a review."

WHEN a customer submits a review, THE system SHALL require a rating (1-5 stars) and a review text of at least 50 characters.

WHILE a review is in draft state, THE system SHALL save it as a temporary draft with auto-saving every 30 seconds.

IF a review text exceeds 2,000 characters, THEN THE system SHALL truncate it to 2,000 characters and display: "Your review has been truncated to 2,000 characters."

IF a customer submits the same review for the same product within 7 days, THEN THE system SHALL reject it with: "You've already submitted a review for this product recently. Please wait 7 days before submitting another."

WHERE seller verification is enabled, THE system SHALL require customers to have completed email verification before submitting reviews.

## Rating System Design

THE system SHALL use a 5-point star rating scale (1 = Poor, 2 = Fair, 3 = Good, 4 = Very Good, 5 = Excellent).

THE system SHALL calculate the overall product rating as the arithmetic mean of all verified ratings.

THE system SHALL display ratings with one decimal place (e.g., 4.3 stars).

WHEN a customer changes their rating on an existing review, THE system SHALL update the average calculation to reflect the new rating.

WHILE product has fewer than 5 reviews, THE system SHALL display: "This product has received too few reviews to be statistically meaningful." before the average rating.

WHEN a product reaches 100+ reviews, THE system SHALL display: "This product has received 100+ verified reviews from real customers." as a badge.

## Review Moderation Process

WHEN a review is flagged by 3 or more users as inappropriate, THE system SHALL automatically queue it for human moderation.

WHEN a moderator approves a flagged review, THE system SHALL remove the flag and restore visibility.

WHEN a moderator rejects a flagged review, THE system SHALL hide it from public view and notify the reviewer: "Your review was removed for violating our community guidelines."

WHILE a review is under moderation, THE system SHALL display: "This review is currently being reviewed by our moderation team." instead of the content.

WHEN a customer receives 3 moderation violations within 30 days, THE system SHALL suspend their review privileges for 90 days.

## Review Verification Rules

WHEN a customer submits a review, THE system SHALL verify that the product purchase has been confirmed as delivered.

WHEN a review is posted, THE system SHALL mark it as "Verified Purchase" if and only if the purchasing account had a confirmed delivery for that specific SKU.

WHERE a customer purchases the same product multiple times with different SKUs, THE system SHALL allow one review per unique SKU purchased.

IF a purchase is refunded or canceled, THEN THE system SHALL immediately hide the associated review and notify the reviewer: "Your review has been hidden because your purchase was refunded."

WHERE a product variant has been discontinued, THE system SHALL allow existing reviews to remain visible but prevent new reviews on that variant.

## Review Display Logic

WHEN viewing a product page, THE system SHALL display reviews sorted by: 1) Verified Purchases first, 2) Highest ratings first, 3) Most recent first.

THE system SHALL group reviews with identical text and ratings as potential duplicates and show a count: "3 similar reviews".

WHEN a review has an image upload, THE system SHALL display the image immediately below the review text.

WHILE a customer is viewing reviews, THE system SHALL show: "X of Y reviews displayed" where X is the number of visible reviews and Y is the total number of reviews.

WHEN a customer filters to show only "Reviews with images", THE system SHALL display only reviews containing images.

IF a review has been marked as "Helpful" by 10 or more users, THEN THE system SHALL display a "Most Helpful" badge.

## Reviewer Identity Protection

THE system SHALL reveal reviewer's first name and first letter of last name only (e.g., "John D.") to other users.

THE system SHALL hide reviewer's exact purchase date and order number from other users.

WHERE a seller responds to a review, THE system SHALL display: "Seller Response" with the seller's store name but NOT their personal name.

WHEN an admin edits or deletes a review, THE system SHALL show: "Edited by Admin" with timestamp and reason for the change.

WHEN a customer requests to delete their reviews, THE system SHALL remove the review content but retain the rating in the overall product
