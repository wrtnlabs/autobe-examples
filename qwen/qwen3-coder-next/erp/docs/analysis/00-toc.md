### Table of Contents

**hrmTracker** is a backend service with the following actors and domain entities.

**Actors**: guest, member
**Entities**: Organization, User, Employee, Role, Permission, Department, Contract, Project, Task, Timelog, Timesheet, ActivityLog, ProjectMember, TaskHistory, Timer, PendingInvitation

---

**Scope**

- **Organization** — has many Employees, Projects, Departments, Contracts, Timelogs, Timesheets, Activities; owned by exactly one User as Owner
- **User** — can belong to multiple Organizations via Employee records; owns zero or more Organizations
- **Employee** — belongs to exactly one Organization; has one current active Contract
- **Role** — assigned to exactly one Employee per Organization
- **Permission** — assigned to Roles
- **Department** — has many Employees; belongs to one Organization
- **Contract** — belongs to one Employee; historical record
- **Project** — has many Tasks and ProjectMembers; belongs to one Organization
- **Task** — belongs to one Project; has TaskHistory entries; can be assigned to one Employee (must be project member)
- **Timelog** — belongs to one Employee and optionally to one Project/Task
- **Timesheet** — contains many Timelogs for one employee and week; belongs to one Organization context
- **ActivityLog** — belongs to an Organization; generated by system actions
- **ProjectMember** — assigns Employee to Project; project-lead has task management rights
- **TaskHistory** — records status change for one Task
- **Timer** — current active timer for one employee; creates Timelog on stop
- **PendingInvitation** — linked to Organization; becomes Employee on signup with matching email

- **guest** (guest)
- **member** (member)
