# EconomicBBS Business Model

## Introduction

EconomicBBS is a streamlined discussion platform focused entirely on economic and political discourse. Designed for simplicity and minimalism, it addresses the rising need for civil, informed conversations free from mainstream social media distractions. This business model defines sustainable growth through ethical revenue streams while maintaining openness and accessibility. By targeting engaged individuals and businesses, EconomicBBS builds a community-first platform generating income through non-intrusive means.

## Revenue Model

The system SHALL generate revenue through two sustainable streams: targeted advertising and premium membership tiers.

### Advertising Revenue

The system SHALL display non-intrusive advertisements from relevant organizations including financial services firms, educational institutions, and industry publications. Up to two ad placements per page (header and sidebar) SHALL be allocated to maintain user experience. The system SHALL support automated ad management through third-party advertising networks for relevant content delivery. Direct ad sales to aligned businesses SHALL supplement this revenue stream. Targets include $5,000 in Year 1 advertising revenue, scaling to $20,000 by Year 2. Key performance metrics SHALL include a minimum 0.5% click-through rate and $1.50 average revenue per thousand impressions (RPM) for the first year.

### Premium Membership Tiers

The system SHALL introduce premium membership tiers upon reaching 5,000 active users. The basic tier SHALL cost $4.99 monthly with ad removal. The advanced tier at $9.99 SHALL include private discussion groups for up to 50 members, monthly expert Q&A sessions, and ad-free article access. The system SHALL track subscription metrics and adjust pricing according to industry benchmarks. Targets include 10% of active users converting to premium within six months of launch and a monthly churn rate below 5%.

## Cost Structure

### Hosting and Infrastructure

The system SHALL utilize scalable cloud infrastructure services with initial costs not exceeding $10 monthly. Resource allocation SHALL scale linearly with user growth while maintaining performance targets.

### Domain Registration

The system SHALL register and automatically renew the domain name economicalbbs.com annually at $12.00 to prevent service disruption.

### Content Moderation

For the initial phase under 5,000 users, community volunteer moderation SHALL suffice. When reaching 10,000 monthly active users, the system SHALL allocate $500 monthly for professional moderation services to ensure compliance with community guidelines and regulatory requirements.

### Image Storage

User upload storage SHALL leverage cloud solutions with 100GB free tier initially. Beyond this threshold, costs SHALL be $0.023 per GB monthly. Data backup systems SHALL be implemented for reliability at an additional 50% storage cost. Upload constraints SHALL optimize storage efficiency while maintaining user engagement.

## Value Proposition

### For Users

The system SHALL provide a clean discussion environment free from mainstream social media distractions. Premium members SHALL enjoy ad-free access. User privacy SHALL be prioritized with no personal data tracking for advertising purposes. The platform SHALL foster a knowledgeable community without algorithmic interference.

### For the Business

The system SHALL generate sustainable revenue through ethical advertising and premium subscriptions. Cost structure SHALL remain minimal, ensuring profitability even with modest user numbers. The user community SHALL serve as a valuable asset for future business opportunities such as market research or institutional partnerships.

## Growth Strategy

### Content-Driven Growth

The system SHALL encourage users to share articles and comments on social platforms such as Twitter and LinkedIn. When users share articles, the system SHALL generate custom links with content snippets and attribution to drive traffic. Targets include a 15% social sharing rate of active users within the first year.

### SEO Optimization

The system SHALL optimize all articles using relevant keywords for search engines. Long-tail economic and political keywords SHALL be targeted to increase organic traffic. Monthly growth of 30% in search traffic SHALL be achieved for the first 12 months.

### Partnerships

The system SHALL form partnerships with university economics departments and think tanks for moderated discussions. Dedicated community areas created for partner topics SHALL be promoted through partner channels to drive targeted traffic and establish credibility.

### Influencer Outreach

The system SHALL engage key influencers in economics and politics through dedicated features such as "Ask Me Anything" sessions. This strategy SHALL drive targeted traffic from influencer follower communities.

### User Referral Program

When a premium member invites a new user who upgrades, the system SHALL credit the referrer with one month of premium membership. This program SHALL be prominently promoted to existing members, with target acquisition costs of $11 per new premium user below industry average of $20.
