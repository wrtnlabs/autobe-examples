# Problem Statement for Economic/Political Discussion Board

## Problem Context

Public discourse on economic and political topics faces significant challenges in the digital environment. Individuals interested in substantive discussions often encounter a majority of platforms that do not prioritize quality debate, thoughtful exchange, or the integrity of information. The current social climate is marked by increasing polarization, misinformation, and an atmosphere where nuanced debate is drowned out by noise, trolling, or aggressive moderation tactics. Users seeking respectful and meaningful engagement, especially around economics, public policy, and politics, struggle to find spaces that are accessible, safe, and focused.

Mainstream social media sites prioritize algorithmic engagement, which fosters virality and sensationalism over depth, often pushing legitimate conversation to the margins. Existing web-based forums may lack modern features, suffer from outdated moderation models, or do not support the seamless sharing of mixed media (articles with images or file attachments), which contributes to unattractive or incomplete experiences for those wanting to share complex information or detailed analysis.

Communities are scattered across niche forums, chat groups, or comment sections of news sites, leading to fragmentation. Users lack a centralized, reliable venue where economic and political content can be discussed with context, civility, and the ability to share supporting material securely.

## User Needs

1. **Open yet Respectful Discourse**:  Users require a platform where they can freely discuss political and economic concerns without fear of harassment, abuse, or arbitrary censorship.
    - THE system SHALL support registered users to post, read, and participate in discussions on economic and political topics.
    - IF a user experiences threatening or abusive messages, THEN THE system SHALL offer tools for reporting and administrative review.

2. **Sharing and Supporting Complex Information**:  Users wish to enrich articles with images, documents, or supporting files, helping to substantiate their arguments with evidence.
    - WHEN a user submits an article, THE system SHALL allow attaching image and file uploads as part of the article content.
    - WHEN another user views an article, THE system SHALL render attached images inline and provide downloadable file links.

3. **Discussion Organization**:  Users need mechanisms for organizing discussions by topic, recency, or popularity to easily find relevant threads.
    - THE system SHALL provide a way to browse, search, and filter articles by recent activity, topic, or featured status.

4. **Content Moderation and Safety**:  Users need a predictable moderation model that prioritizes fairness, transparency, and the prevention of abuse/spam without suppressing valid speech.
    - WHEN a user reports inappropriate content, THE system SHALL forward the report to administrators for review.
    - WHEN an administrator reviews reported content, THE system SHALL allow removal, editing, or restoration of any article, comment, or attachment as needed for compliance.

5. **User Empowerment and Self-Management**: Registered users expect to control their own submissions and identities.
    - WHEN a user wishes to edit or delete their own article, THE system SHALL enable this, subject to configured time or policy constraints.
    - THE system SHALL require authentication before letting any user create, edit, or delete discussion content.


## Current Market Gaps

1. **Algorithmic Misdirection & Sensationalism**:  Conventional social platforms amplify extreme views and discourage depth, leading to incoherent and polarizing discussions where reasoned debate is disincentivized. Users seeking substance often leave or disengage.
2. **Weak Quality Control**: Many open discussion spaces fail to implement effective moderation for hate speech, misinformation, or personal attacks, creating hostile environments.
3. **Limited Attachment Functionality**: Most political/economic forums do not natively support robust image/file attachments, which restricts nuanced exchanges based on evidence or analysis.
4. **Fragmentation & Lack of Specialization**: Few current solutions exist exclusively for economic and political discussion, causing interested users to fragment among general-purpose sites or isolated communities with little cross-pollination, discoverability, or reliability.
5. **Onerous Registration or Privacy Concerns**: Some platforms have rigid onboarding, excessive data collection, or non-transparent privacy practices that discourage new members or threaten anonymity necessary for open discourse.

## Opportunity

A simple and focused discussion board aimed at economic and political discourse presents a unique opportunity to fill these gaps by prioritizing:

- **Substance over Sensation**: By offering a straightforward, single-purpose venue with discussion threads, comment support, and robust attachment capability, the platform can attract and retain contributors seeking depth and evidence-rich engagement.

- **Predictable Moderation and Civility**: Clearly defined moderation policies and transparent administration encourage trust, safety, and consistent community standards, attracting those tired of arbitrary censorship or unchecked abuse.

- **Ease of Use and Accessibility**: Minimal onboarding, clear permissions for both regular users and administrators, and support for multimedia attachments will foster vibrant conversation, lowering the technical barrier to participation.

- **Community-Centric Value**: By centralizing economic and political conversations, the platform amplifies the voices of informed citizens, policy enthusiasts, students, professionals, and activists. This leads to greater information density, better resource sharing, and heightened impact in public discourse.

- **Sustainable Growth**: Respecting user privacy and enabling administrators to directly intervene in disputes, block abusers, and enforce policy without excess bureaucracy allows for healthy growth and sustained relevance.

A successful implementation will be measured by:
- Increase in high-quality, evidence-backed content
- Positive user feedback on the fairness and clarity of moderation
- Engagement rates indicating sustainable thread creation and participation
- Rate of successful file and image uploads alongside articles

By directly addressing these user-centric problems and gaps, the system becomes a vital tool for those who want to elevate the level of economic and political discussion online, supporting both freedom of expression and community standards of civility.
