# Service Overview: Economic and Political Discussion Board

## Service Vision and Purpose

The Economic and Political Discussion Board is a focused online platform designed to facilitate thoughtful, substantive conversations about economic and political topics. This service provides a dedicated space where individuals interested in these subjects can share articles, engage in meaningful discussions, and exchange perspectives in a structured environment.

**Primary Mission**: To create a straightforward, accessible platform where users can publish articles on economic and political topics, attach relevant supporting materials, and participate in constructive discussions through comments.

**Core Philosophy**: Simplicity and focus. Unlike complex social media platforms or feature-heavy forum software, this discussion board deliberately maintains a minimal, essential feature set. The service prioritizes clarity of discussion over bells and whistles, ensuring that the content and conversations remain central.

**Service Essence**: At its heart, this is a publication and discussion platform where:
- Users can write and share articles on economic and political subjects
- Authors can attach images and files to support their arguments and provide evidence
- Community members can comment and engage in threaded discussions
- Moderators ensure discussions remain civil and substantive

## Problem Statement

### The Gap in Current Discussion Platforms

Modern internet users interested in economic and political discourse face several challenges with existing platforms:

**Noise and Distraction**: Large social media platforms mix economic and political content with entertainment, personal updates, and advertisements, making focused discussion difficult. Users seeking substantive policy analysis or economic theory discussion must wade through irrelevant content.

**Complexity Overload**: Many existing forum platforms have become bloated with features that distract from core discussion purposes. Users face steep learning curves with unnecessary gamification, complex reputation systems, and confusing navigation structures.

**Lack of Supporting Evidence**: Simple comment-based platforms don't allow users to easily attach supporting documents, data files, charts, or reference images when making arguments about economic policies or political positions. This limits the depth and quality of discussions.

**Fragmented Communities**: Economic and political discussions are scattered across general-purpose platforms, making it hard to find a consistent community of engaged, knowledgeable participants interested in these specific topics.

### Pain Points This Service Addresses

**FOR CONTENT CREATORS**: Individuals who want to share thoughtful analysis of economic policies, political developments, or theoretical perspectives need a platform where they can:
- Write comprehensive articles without character limits
- Attach charts, graphs, and data files to support their arguments
- Include reference documents and images
- Receive structured feedback through comments

**FOR DISCUSSION PARTICIPANTS**: People who want to engage with economic and political content need:
- A dedicated space focused specifically on these topics
- The ability to read well-supported arguments with attached evidence
- A simple, distraction-free interface for reading and commenting
- Moderation that maintains discussion quality without censoring diverse viewpoints

**FOR COMMUNITY MODERATORS**: Those responsible for maintaining discussion quality need:
- Clear tools to manage inappropriate content
- Simple moderation workflows without complex features
- The ability to maintain community standards efficiently

## Target Audience and Users

### Primary User Personas

**THE INFORMED CITIZEN** (Core User)
- Actively interested in understanding economic policies and political developments
- Reads news and analysis regularly
- Wants to engage with others who share this interest
- May have professional or academic background, or simply be self-educated
- Values evidence-based discussion over rhetoric
- Seeks to deepen understanding through diverse perspectives

**THE ANALYST/WRITER** (Content Creator)
- Has insights, analysis, or perspectives to share on economic or political topics
- Wants to publish longer-form content than social media allows
- Needs to attach supporting materials (charts, data, research papers)
- Desires structured feedback and discussion from engaged readers
- May be a student, professional, researcher, or informed enthusiast

**THE CASUAL LEARNER** (Occasional Participant)
- Interested in economic and political topics but not deeply engaged
- Primarily reads articles and discussions rather than creating content
- May occasionally comment when particularly moved by content
- Benefits from exposure to well-reasoned arguments and supporting evidence

### Why These Users Choose This Platform

**FOCUS AND RELEVANCE**: Every article and discussion is about economic or political topics, eliminating the noise found on general platforms.

**EVIDENCE-BASED DISCOURSE**: The ability to attach files and images encourages users to support their arguments with data, research, and visual evidence.

**SIMPLICITY**: Users don't need to learn complex features or navigate confusing interfaces. The platform does exactly what they need without overwhelming them.

**QUALITY MODERATION**: Active moderation ensures discussions remain substantive and civil without becoming echo chambers.

**DEDICATED COMMUNITY**: Users find others who share their interest in serious economic and political discussion.

## Core Value Proposition

### Unique Benefits

**SIMPLICITY MEETS SUBSTANCE**
While other platforms add complexity, this discussion board strips away everything except what matters: quality content, supporting evidence, and meaningful discussion. Users get a clean, focused experience that respects their time and intelligence.

**EVIDENCE-FIRST DISCUSSIONS**
By enabling image and file attachments directly on articles, the platform encourages evidence-based argumentation. Users can attach:
- Economic data and statistics in spreadsheet format
- Charts and graphs showing trends
- Research papers and policy documents (PDF attachments)
- Historical images and infographics
- Source documents for fact-checking

This transforms discussions from opinion exchanges into evidence-based debates.

**ACCESSIBLE DEPTH**
The platform serves both expert contributors and curious learners. The simple interface welcomes newcomers while the attachment capabilities satisfy those wanting to dive deep into complex topics.

**CURATED QUALITY**
Moderation maintains community standards without requiring complex algorithms or automated systems. Human moderators ensure discussions remain on-topic and respectful.

### Key Differentiators

**vs. General Social Media**: Focused exclusively on economic and political discussion, free from personal updates, entertainment, and advertising noise.

**vs. Complex Forums**: Simple, straightforward interface without reputation systems, badges, complicated user levels, or feature bloat.

**vs. Comment Platforms**: Support for file and image attachments enables evidence-based discussion rather than unsupported opinions.

**vs. Academic Platforms**: Accessible to non-academics while still supporting rigorous, evidence-based discussion.

## Business Model

### Service Sustainability Strategy

**INITIAL PHASE: Community Building (Months 1-12)**

The first year focuses on establishing a quality user base and proven value proposition. During this phase:

- The service operates with minimal costs (hosting, domain, basic infrastructure)
- No monetization required initially to avoid compromising user experience
- Focus on organic growth through word-of-mouth from satisfied users
- Build reputation for quality discussions and evidence-based dialogue

**GROWTH PHASE: Value Demonstration (Year 2+)**

Once a consistent, engaged user base is established:

**POTENTIAL REVENUE MODEL 1: Premium Features (Freemium)**
- Basic posting and discussion remains free for all members
- Premium tier ($5-10/month) offers:
  - Larger file attachment limits (free tier: 10MB per file, premium: 100MB)
  - More attachments per article (free tier: 3 files, premium: 15 files)
  - Extended article archive access
  - Advanced search capabilities
  - Priority support

**POTENTIAL REVENUE MODEL 2: Institutional Subscriptions**
- Educational institutions, think tanks, or research organizations pay for team access
- Organizational accounts with multiple seats
- Enhanced features for institutional users (analytics, collaboration tools)
- Pricing: $50-200/month depending on team size

**POTENTIAL REVENUE MODEL 3: Ethical Advertising (If Necessary)**
- Minimal, non-intrusive text-based advertisements
- Strictly limited to relevant services (publications, courses, research tools)
- No tracking or behavioral advertising
- Clear labeling of sponsored content
- User option to remove ads with premium subscription

**ALTERNATIVE: Community Funding**
- Voluntary donations from users who value the platform
- Patreon or similar community funding model
- Transparent reporting of costs and funding
- Sustainability through community support rather than advertising

### Growth Strategy

**ORGANIC ACQUISITION**
- Quality content attracts users through search engines and social sharing
- Word-of-mouth from satisfied users interested in economic and political topics
- Strategic partnerships with educational institutions or policy organizations

**RETENTION FOCUS**
- Simple, reliable platform keeps users coming back
- Quality moderation maintains discussion standards
- Regular engagement from active community members

**SCALABILITY APPROACH**
- Start small with focused community
- Scale infrastructure gradually as user base grows
- Maintain simplicity even as platform scales
- Avoid feature creep that compromises core value proposition

### Cost Structure

**MINIMAL OPERATING COSTS**
- Cloud hosting (scales with usage): $20-100/month initially
- Domain and SSL certificates: $50/year
- Basic infrastructure and CDN for file storage: $50-200/month
- Moderation (volunteer community moderators initially, paid if service grows)

The service is designed to operate efficiently with low overhead, making sustainability achievable even with modest revenue or community funding.

## Success Metrics

### Primary Success Indicators

**USER ENGAGEMENT METRICS**

**Active Users**: 
- Daily Active Users (DAU): Target 100+ within 6 months, 500+ within 1 year
- Monthly Active Users (MAU): Target 500+ within 6 months, 2,000+ within 1 year
- DAU/MAU ratio above 20% indicates strong engagement

**Content Creation**:
- New articles published per week: Target 20+ within 6 months
- Articles with file/image attachments: Target 60%+ attachment rate
- Average article length: Target 500+ words indicating substantive content

**Discussion Quality**:
- Comments per article average: Target 5+ comments per article
- Comment response rate: Target 40%+ of comments receiving replies
- Evidence attachment rate in discussions: Track how often users attach supporting files

**COMMUNITY HEALTH METRICS**

**Content Moderation**:
- Moderation response time: Target under 24 hours for reported content
- Percentage of content requiring moderation: Target under 5% (indicates healthy community norms)
- User appeals upheld: Track fairness of moderation decisions

**User Retention**:
- 30-day retention rate: Target 40%+ (users who return within 30 days)
- 90-day retention rate: Target 25%+ (indicates long-term value)
- Contributor retention: Track percentage of article authors who publish multiple times

**PLATFORM PERFORMANCE**

**Technical Reliability**:
- Platform uptime: Target 99.5%+ availability
- Page load times: Target under 2 seconds for article pages
- File upload success rate: Target 99%+ for supported file sizes

**User Satisfaction**:
- User-reported issues: Track and trend support requests
- Feature requests: Monitor what users ask for to validate simplicity approach
- Net Promoter Score (if surveyed): Target 40+ among active users

### Leading Indicators of Success

**EARLY SIGNALS (First 3 Months)**
- Growing article publication rate (indicates content creator acquisition)
- Rising comment activity (indicates discussion engagement)
- Return visitor percentage above 30% (indicates value delivery)
- Low bounce rate on article pages (indicates content quality)

**VALIDATION SIGNALS (Months 3-12)**
- Organic growth through search and referrals (indicates product-market fit)
- Established contributor base publishing regularly (indicates creator satisfaction)
- Healthy discussion threads with evidence attachments (indicates platform value realization)
- Low churn among active members (indicates retention of quality users)

## Service Scope and Boundaries

### Core Features (IN SCOPE)

**ARTICLE PUBLISHING SYSTEM**
- Members can create and publish articles on economic and political topics
- Rich text editing for article content
- Image attachment capability (multiple images per article)
- File attachment capability (PDFs, spreadsheets, documents)
- Article editing by original author
- Article deletion by author or moderators

**DISCUSSION AND COMMENTING**
- Members can comment on published articles
- Threaded comment discussions
- Comment editing by original commenter
- Comment deletion by commenter or moderators
- Simple chronological comment ordering

**USER MANAGEMENT**
- Guest browsing of published articles (read-only)
- Member registration and authentication
- Member login and session management
- Basic user profiles (name, bio, joined date)
- Password reset capability

**CONTENT MODERATION**
- Moderator review of reported content
- Moderator ability to remove inappropriate articles
- Moderator ability to remove inappropriate comments
- Simple content reporting by members
- Moderation action logging

**CONTENT DISCOVERY**
- Browse recent articles (chronological listing)
- Basic search functionality (search by title and content)
- Simple categorization or tagging (economic vs political topics)

### Intentionally Excluded (OUT OF SCOPE)

**TO MAINTAIN SIMPLICITY**

**NO Complex Social Features**:
- No user reputation systems or karma points
- No user following or friend systems
- No direct messaging between users
- No user badges or achievements
- No gamification elements

**NO Advanced Content Features**:
- No article versioning or revision history
- No collaborative editing or co-authoring
- No article drafts or scheduled publishing
- No rich media embedding (beyond simple image attachments)
- No polls, surveys, or interactive elements

**NO Complex Moderation**:
- No automated content filtering or AI moderation
- No complex user warning systems
- No temporary bans or suspension tiers
- No appeal workflows beyond simple moderator review

**NO Advanced Discovery**:
- No recommendation algorithms
- No trending or hot topics
- No complex filtering or sorting options
- No user-curated collections or lists
- No advanced analytics or metrics for users

**NO Monetization Features (Initially)**:
- No advertising systems
- No premium tiers or paid features
- No donation or tipping functionality
- No subscription management

### Clear Boundaries

**FOCUS ON CORE MISSION**: Every feature decision is evaluated against the question: "Does this directly support publishing articles, attaching evidence, and facilitating discussion?" If not, it's out of scope.

**SIMPLICITY OVER FEATURES**: When choosing between adding a feature or keeping the platform simple, simplicity wins. This platform succeeds by doing a few things well, not many things adequately.

**MANUAL OVER AUTOMATED**: Where possible, use simple manual processes (like moderator review) rather than building complex automated systems. This keeps development focused and maintenance minimal.

**EVIDENCE-BASED DISCUSSIONS**: The platform supports evidence through file and image attachments. This is a core differentiator and remains in scope. Features that don't support evidence-based discussion are deprioritized.
