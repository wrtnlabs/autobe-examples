# Service Overview: Economic/Political Discussion Board

## Introduction

This document provides a comprehensive overview of a web-based discussion board platform designed specifically for economic and political discourse. The platform enables users to publish articles, attach supporting materials (images and documents), and engage in structured discussions through a commenting system. This overview establishes the business foundation, target audience, value proposition, and success criteria that guide all subsequent technical requirements.

## Service Vision and Purpose

### Why This Service Exists

The economic and political discussion board exists to address a critical gap in online discourse platforms: the need for a simple, focused space where substantive discussions about economic policy, political theory, and socio-economic issues can occur without the noise and complexity of general-purpose social media platforms.

**Primary Problems Solved:**

1. **Information Fragmentation**: Economic and political discussions are currently scattered across general social media platforms, news site comment sections, and complex forum software, making it difficult to build coherent, ongoing conversations.

2. **Content Depth Limitations**: Twitter/X-style platforms limit nuanced discussion with character restrictions, while traditional blog platforms lack community engagement features.

3. **Barrier to Entry**: Existing discussion platforms often require technical expertise to set up and manage, or come bundled with unnecessary features that complicate the user experience.

4. **Evidence-Based Discussion**: Most platforms make it difficult to attach supporting documentation, research papers, charts, or data visualizations directly to discussion posts, hindering evidence-based discourse.

**Core Mission:**

To provide a streamlined, accessible platform where individuals interested in economic and political topics can share well-researched articles, support their arguments with visual and documentary evidence, and engage in meaningful dialogue without technical barriers or feature bloat.

### Business Justification

This service operates on the principle that specialized, focused platforms serve niche communities better than generalized social networks. The economic/political discussion space represents a valuable market segment:

**Market Opportunity:**
- Policy researchers, academics, students, journalists, and engaged citizens need dedicated spaces for substantive discourse
- Educational institutions seek platforms for political science and economics coursework discussions
- Think tanks and policy organizations require community engagement tools
- Independent analysts and commentators need publishing platforms with community features

**Competitive Landscape:**
- **General Social Media** (Twitter, Facebook, Reddit): Too noisy, algorithm-driven, lacks focus on long-form content
- **Blogging Platforms** (Medium, WordPress): Strong on publishing, weak on community discussion features
- **Traditional Forums** (phpBB, vBulletin): Outdated interfaces, overly complex feature sets
- **Specialized Academic Platforms**: Often closed or require institutional access

**Differentiation Strategy:**
This platform sits in the sweet spot between blogging and forum software, offering article publishing with rich media support while maintaining discussion functionality, all within a simple, modern interface focused exclusively on economic and political content.

## Business Model and Justification

### Revenue Strategy

While this platform prioritizes simplicity and community value, sustainable operation requires a viable business model. The following strategies support long-term viability:

**Phase 1: Community Building (Months 0-12)**
- **Free Access Model**: All core features available to registered users at no cost
- **Objective**: Build critical mass of quality content and active user base
- **Metric**: Achieve 1,000+ registered users, 500+ published articles, 5,000+ comments
- **Funding**: Initial development and operation funded through founder investment or grants

**Phase 2: Sustainability (Months 12-24)**
- **Freemium Model Introduction**:
  - Basic access remains free (read all content, limited posting)
  - Premium membership ($5-10/month) offers enhanced features:
    - Unlimited article posting (free tier: 2 articles/month)
    - Increased attachment limits (free tier: 3 files per article, premium: 10 files)
    - Priority support and enhanced profile features
    - Ad-free experience (if ads introduced)
- **Institutional Subscriptions**: Universities, think tanks, research organizations ($50-200/month)
  - Team accounts with collaborative features
  - Analytics on content engagement
  - Branded subgroups for institutional discussions

**Phase 3: Growth and Diversification (Months 24+)**
- **Premium Content Partnerships**: Allow authors to publish premium articles for subscribers
- **Sponsored Categories**: Policy organizations or educational institutions sponsor topic areas
- **Data Access**: Anonymized discussion trend data for academic research (with user consent)
- **Educational Licensing**: Package platform for classroom use with instructor tools

**Alternative/Complementary Revenue Streams:**
- **Grants and Foundations**: Apply for civic engagement, education, or media innovation grants
- **Non-Profit Model**: Operate as 501(c)(3) with donation-based funding if mission-driven
- **Ethical Advertising**: Minimal, non-intrusive ads from policy organizations, educational institutions, or relevant publications (if freemium insufficient)

### Cost Structure

**Development Costs:**
- Initial development: Backend architecture, user authentication, content management, file storage
- Estimated development time: 2-4 months for MVP with core features
- Ongoing maintenance and feature enhancements: Continuous but minimal given simplicity focus

**Operating Costs:**
- **Infrastructure**: Cloud hosting, database services, file storage (estimated $50-500/month depending on scale)
- **Domain and SSL**: Minimal annual costs ($20-50/year)
- **Moderation**: Initially volunteer/founder-led, scale to part-time moderators as community grows
- **Support**: Email-based user support, initially low volume

**Scaling Economics:**
- Cloud infrastructure scales with usage (pay-as-you-grow model)
- File storage costs grow linearly with content but remain manageable with reasonable attachment limits
- Moderation needs grow with user base but simplified platform design minimizes abuse vectors

### Success Sustainability

The business model balances several key principles:

1. **Simplicity Preservation**: Monetization strategies must not compromise the core value of simplicity
2. **Community First**: Revenue generation serves community sustainability, not investor returns
3. **Aligned Incentives**: Premium features enhance creator and power-user experience without creating content paywalls
4. **Scalable Economics**: Costs grow predictably with usage, allowing gradual revenue scaling

## Success Criteria and Metrics

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

**User Growth Metrics:**
- **Registered Users**: Track total registered accounts and monthly active users (MAU)
  - Target: 1,000 users in first year, 5,000 in second year
- **User Retention**: Percentage of users who return within 30 days of registration
  - Target: 40% 30-day retention rate
- **User Engagement**: Average sessions per user per month
  - Target: 8-12 sessions/month for active users

**Content Metrics:**
- **Articles Published**: Total articles and monthly publication rate
  - Target: 500 articles in first year, growth to 2,000+ in second year
- **Article Quality**: Average article length, attachment usage rate
  - Target: 60% of articles include at least one attachment
- **Comment Activity**: Average comments per article, total comments
  - Target: 5-10 comments per article average

**Engagement Metrics:**
- **Read-Through Rate**: Percentage of articles read beyond first paragraph
  - Target: 60%+ read-through rate indicating quality content
- **Discussion Depth**: Average comment thread length, response rates
  - Target: 3+ comment depth on 40% of articles
- **Search and Discovery**: Percentage of users using search, category browsing
  - Target: 50% of sessions include search or category browsing

**Community Health Metrics:**
- **Moderation Load**: Reports per 1,000 articles, moderator action rate
  - Target: Low moderation needs (<2% of content requiring action)
- **User Satisfaction**: Through periodic surveys or feedback mechanisms
  - Target: 80%+ satisfaction with platform simplicity and functionality
- **Content Diversity**: Distribution of articles across economic/political categories
  - Target: No single category exceeds 40% of content (healthy topic diversity)

**Business Metrics:**
- **Conversion Rate**: Percentage of free users converting to premium (if freemium model)
  - Target: 5-10% conversion rate for freemium model
- **Revenue Per User**: Average revenue generated per active user
  - Target: $2-5 per active user annually
- **Operational Efficiency**: Infrastructure costs as percentage of revenue
  - Target: Infrastructure costs <30% of revenue at scale

## Core Features Overview

The platform consists of four primary functional areas, each designed with simplicity as the guiding principle:

### 1. User Management and Authentication

**Registration and Login:**
- WHEN a visitor chooses to register, THE system SHALL collect email address and password
- THE system SHALL verify email addresses before allowing article posting
- WHEN a user logs in, THE system SHALL maintain their session using JWT tokens for security
- Users can reset forgotten passwords through email-based recovery

**User Actors:**
- **Guests**: Unauthenticated visitors who can browse and read all public articles and comments
- **Members**: Registered users who can create articles, post comments, and manage their own content
- **Moderators**: Trusted users with elevated permissions to manage all content and enforce community guidelines

**Profile Management:**
- THE system SHALL store basic user profile information (display name, bio, profile picture)
- WHEN users edit their profiles, THE system SHALL update information immediately
- Users can view their posting history and comment activity
- Users can delete their accounts and associated data upon request

### 2. Article Publishing and Management

**Article Creation:**
- WHEN a member creates an article, THE system SHALL require a title and body content
- THE system SHALL support rich text formatting for article bodies (headings, bold, italic, lists, links)
- WHEN composing articles, THE system SHALL allow attachment of images and document files
- THE system SHALL support up to 5 attachments per article with maximum 10MB per file
- Users can categorize articles using predefined economic/political categories
- Users can add tags to articles for improved discoverability

**Supported Attachments:**
- **Images**: JPEG, PNG, GIF formats for charts, infographics, photographs
- **Documents**: PDF, DOCX formats for research papers, policy documents, data tables

**Article Management:**
- WHEN a member views their own article, THE system SHALL provide edit and delete options
- THE system SHALL allow article editing within 24 hours of publication for minor corrections
- THE system SHALL allow article deletion by the author or moderators at any time
- IF an article is deleted, THEN THE system SHALL remove it from public view but maintain moderation records

**Article Display:**
- THE system SHALL display articles in a clean, readable format with clear typography
- Attached images display inline within articles or as a gallery
- Attached documents appear as downloadable links with file type and size indicated
- THE system SHALL show article metadata: author, publication date, category, tags

### 3. Discussion and Comments

**Comment Functionality:**
- WHEN a member views an article, THE system SHALL provide the ability to post comments
- THE system SHALL display comments in chronological order below the article
- WHEN users post comments, THE system SHALL support basic text formatting
- THE system SHALL allow members to edit or delete their own comments
- Moderators can edit or delete any comment to enforce community guidelines

**Comment Structure:**
- Comments are single-level (non-threaded) to maintain simplicity
- Each comment displays author name, timestamp, and content
- THE system SHALL limit comments to 2,000 characters to encourage focused responses
- No upvoting, downvoting, or other engagement metrics on comments

**Comment Moderation:**
- WHEN users encounter inappropriate comments, THE system SHALL provide a reporting mechanism
- WHEN content is reported, THE system SHALL notify moderators for review
- Moderators can remove comments or issue warnings to users violating guidelines

### 4. Search, Discovery, and Navigation

**Content Browsing:**
- THE system SHALL display recent articles on the homepage in reverse chronological order
- WHEN users browse categories, THE system SHALL show all articles in that category
- THE system SHALL paginate article listings showing 20 articles per page
- Users can filter by category or tag to narrow content discovery

**Search Functionality:**
- THE system SHALL provide keyword search across article titles and body content
- WHEN users search, THE system SHALL return results instantly for common queries (within 2 seconds)
- Search results display article title, author, excerpt, and publication date
- THE system SHALL highlight search terms in results for easier relevance assessment

**Category Organization:**
- Predefined categories include: Economic Policy, Political Theory, Trade and Commerce, Public Finance, Labor and Employment, International Relations, Domestic Policy, Regulatory Affairs
- THE system SHALL allow articles to belong to one primary category
- Tags provide additional granularity beyond categories (e.g., "monetary policy," "healthcare reform," "climate policy")

## User Journey Overview

### Guest User Journey

**Discovery and Exploration:**
1. Guest visits the platform through web search, social media link, or direct referral
2. Homepage displays recent articles with titles, authors, and excerpts
3. Guest can browse categories or use search to find topics of interest
4. Guest clicks on an article to read full content including attachments
5. Guest reads comments and discussion below the article
6. IF guest wants to participate, THEN THE system SHALL prompt registration

**Conversion to Member:**
- WHEN a guest decides to register, THE system SHALL present a simple registration form
- Guest provides email address and password
- THE system SHALL send email verification link
- WHEN guest clicks verification link, THE system SHALL activate the account
- New member can immediately log in and access full platform features

### Member User Journey

**Content Creation Flow:**
1. Member logs in using email and password
2. Member navigates to "Create Article" interface
3. Member enters article title and composes body content with formatting
4. Member selects primary category and adds relevant tags
5. Member uploads image attachments (charts, graphs, photos) and/or document files (PDFs, DOCX)
6. Member previews the article to ensure formatting and attachments display correctly
7. Member publishes the article
8. THE system SHALL display the published article immediately and list it in relevant category

**Engagement Flow:**
1. Member browses homepage or searches for articles of interest
2. Member reads article and reviews attached documents or images
3. Member scrolls to comment section to read existing discussion
4. Member posts a thoughtful comment engaging with article content or other commenters
5. THE system SHALL display the new comment immediately
6. Member receives notifications when others reply to their articles or comments (optional feature)

**Content Management Flow:**
1. Member views their profile to see list of published articles and comments
2. Member clicks on an article they authored
3. IF within 24 hours of publication, THE member can edit the article to fix errors
4. Member can delete their own articles at any time
5. THE system SHALL confirm deletion before permanently removing content

### Moderator User Journey

**Content Review Flow:**
1. Moderator logs in with moderator credentials
2. Moderator accesses moderation dashboard showing reported content
3. Moderator reviews reported article or comment in context
4. Moderator determines if content violates community guidelines
5. IF content is inappropriate, THEN moderator removes it and logs the action
6. IF content is acceptable, THEN moderator dismisses the report
7. THE system SHALL maintain audit log of all moderation actions

**Proactive Moderation:**
1. Moderator browses recent articles and comments
2. Moderator identifies content that may violate guidelines even without reports
3. Moderator takes appropriate action (warning, edit, removal)
4. THE system SHALL notify the content author of moderation action when appropriate

## System Scope and Boundaries

### In Scope - Features Included

**User Features:**
- User registration, authentication, and session management
- Email verification and password recovery
- Basic user profiles with display name, bio, and profile picture
- User posting history and activity view

**Content Features:**
- Article creation with rich text formatting
- Image and document file attachments (up to 5 files per article, 10MB each)
- Article categorization and tagging
- Article editing (within 24 hours) and deletion
- Comment posting, editing, and deletion
- Simple chronological comment display

**Discovery Features:**
- Homepage with recent articles
- Category-based browsing
- Tag-based filtering
- Keyword search across article titles and content
- Pagination for article listings

**Moderation Features:**
- Content reporting by users
- Moderator dashboard for reviewing reports
- Moderator actions: edit, delete articles and comments
- Moderation action logging

**Administrative Features:**
- User role assignment (member to moderator)
- Basic user account management
- Content overview and statistics

### Out of Scope - Features Explicitly Excluded

**Social Features NOT Included:**
- No follower/following system
- No direct messaging between users
- No user-to-user notifications beyond standard comment replies
- No social sharing buttons or integration
- No like/upvote/downvote systems
- No reputation scores or gamification

**Advanced Content Features NOT Included:**
- No threaded/nested comment replies (comments are single-level only)
- No article co-authoring or collaboration features
- No draft saving or scheduled publishing
- No article revision history
- No multimedia embedding beyond file attachments (no embedded videos, tweets, etc.)
- No real-time collaborative editing

**Discovery Features NOT Included:**
- No algorithmic content recommendations
- No trending/popular content rankings
- No personalized content feeds
- No advanced search filters (date ranges, author search beyond basic keyword)

**Monetization Features NOT Initially Included:**
- No built-in payment processing
- No premium content paywalls
- No advertising system (may be added later if needed)
- No subscription management (may be added in Phase 2)

**Technical Features NOT Included:**
- No mobile native applications (web-responsive only)
- No API for third-party integrations
- No export tools beyond basic account data download
- No advanced analytics or insights dashboard for users

### Technical Boundaries

**Platform Scope:**
- Web-based application accessible through modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Responsive design for mobile and tablet access through mobile browsers
- No native mobile applications in initial release

**File Handling Boundaries:**
- Maximum 5 attachments per article
- Maximum 10MB per individual file
- Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, GIF for images; PDF, DOCX for documents
- No video or audio file support
- No specialized file viewers (PDFs and DOCX download for external viewing)

**Scale Expectations:**
- Platform designed for 1,000-10,000 users in first two years
- Optimized for hundreds of articles per month, not thousands per day
- Moderation assumes manageable community scale (not millions of users)

## Non-Functional Requirements Overview

### Performance Requirements

**Response Time Expectations:**
- WHEN users navigate between pages, THE system SHALL load pages within 2 seconds under normal conditions
- WHEN users submit articles or comments, THE system SHALL process and display the content within 3 seconds
- WHEN users perform searches, THE system SHALL return results within 2 seconds for common queries
- WHEN users upload files, THE system SHALL provide progress feedback and complete uploads within reasonable time based on file size and connection speed

**Availability:**
- THE system SHALL maintain 99% uptime during business hours (measured monthly)
- Planned maintenance should occur during low-traffic periods with advance notice
- THE system SHALL recover from failures within 1 hour for critical issues

**Scalability:**
- THE system SHALL support at least 100 concurrent users without performance degradation
- THE system SHALL handle up to 1,000 registered users and 10,000 articles in the first year
- Infrastructure shall scale to accommodate growth in Phase 2 (up to 10,000 users)

**Mobile Responsiveness:**

### Maintainability Requirements
