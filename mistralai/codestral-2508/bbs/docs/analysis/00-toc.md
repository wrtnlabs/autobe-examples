# Economic/Political Discussion Board Service Overview

## Service Vision

The Economic/Political Discussion Board is a platform designed to facilitate informed discussions and debates on economic and political topics. Our mission is to provide a space where users can share their insights, exchange ideas, and engage in meaningful conversations about the issues that matter most to them.

## Problem Statement

In today's world, there is a growing need for a platform that allows users to discuss economic and political topics in a respectful and informed manner. Existing platforms often lack the depth and focus required for such discussions, and many are plagued by misinformation and divisive rhetoric. Our service aims to address these issues by providing a platform that fosters informed discussions and promotes a culture of respect and understanding.

## Target Audience

### Primary Users

- **General Public**: Individuals interested in economic and political topics who seek to engage in informed discussions and debates.
- **Economic Analysts**: Professionals who analyze economic trends and policies and wish to share their insights with a broader audience.
- **Political Commentators**: Individuals who comment on political events and developments and wish to share their perspectives with others.

### Secondary Users

- **Moderators**: Individuals responsible for maintaining the platform and ensuring that discussions remain respectful and on-topic.
- **Administrators**: Individuals responsible for the overall management and operation of the platform.

## Core Features

### User Authentication

- Users can register and log in to the platform using their email address and password.
- Users can reset their password if they forget it.

### Article Management

- Users can create, edit, and delete their own articles.
- Users can view articles created by other users.
- Users can search for articles based on keywords and topics.

### Comment Management

- Users can add comments to articles.
- Users can edit and delete their own comments.
- Users can report comments that violate the platform's guidelines.

### File and Image Attachments

- Users can attach files and images to their articles and comments.
- Users can view and download files and images attached to articles and comments.

### User Permissions

- **Guests**: Unauthenticated users who can only view public articles and register/login.
- **Members**: Authenticated users who can create, edit, and delete their own articles and comments.
- **Moderators**: Administrators who can manage all articles, comments, and user accounts.

## Success Metrics

- **User Engagement**: The number of active users and the frequency of their interactions with the platform.
- **Content Quality**: The quality and relevance of the articles and comments posted on the platform.
- **User Satisfaction**: The level of satisfaction of users with the platform and its features.
- **Revenue Generation**: The amount of revenue generated from the platform, if applicable.
