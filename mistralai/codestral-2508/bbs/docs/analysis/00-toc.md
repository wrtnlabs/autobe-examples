# Economic Discussion Board Service Overview

## Service Vision

The Economic Discussion Board is a platform designed to facilitate informed discussions and knowledge sharing among users interested in economic and political topics. The service aims to provide a space where users can exchange ideas, share insights, and engage in meaningful conversations about economic and political issues.

## Problem Statement

There is a lack of a dedicated platform for users to discuss economic and political topics in a structured and organized manner. Existing platforms often lack the specific features and focus required for such discussions, leading to fragmented and disorganized conversations. The Economic Discussion Board aims to address this gap by providing a specialized platform for economic and political discussions.

## Target Audience

### Primary Users

- **Economists**: Professionals and researchers interested in economic theories, policies, and trends.
- **Political Analysts**: Individuals who analyze political events, policies, and trends.
- **General Public**: Users interested in economic and political topics who wish to stay informed and engage in discussions.

### Secondary Users

- **Content Creators**: Users who contribute articles, posts, and comments to the platform.
- **Moderators**: Users responsible for managing the content and ensuring a positive and respectful environment.

## Key Features

- **User Registration and Authentication**: Users can register and log in to the platform to access all features.
- **Post Creation and Management**: Users can create, edit, and delete their own posts.
- **Commenting System**: Users can comment on posts to engage in discussions.
- **Attachment Uploads**: Users can upload images and files to their posts.
- **Search and Filtering**: Users can search for specific topics and filter posts by categories or tags.
- **Notification System**: Users receive notifications for new comments and replies.

## Success Metrics

- **User Engagement**: The number of active users and the frequency of posts and comments.
- **Content Quality**: The quality and relevance of the content posted on the platform.
- **User Satisfaction**: Feedback from users regarding their experience on the platform.
