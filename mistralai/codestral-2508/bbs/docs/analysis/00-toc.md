# Economic/Political Discussion Board Service Overview

## Service Vision

The Economic/Political Discussion Board is a platform designed to facilitate informed discussions on economic and political topics. Our mission is to provide a space where users can share insights, exchange ideas, and engage in meaningful conversations about the economic and political landscape.

## Problem Statement

In today's complex economic and political environment, there is a need for a platform that allows users to discuss and analyze these topics in a structured and informed manner. Existing platforms often lack the depth and focus required for such discussions, leading to fragmented and sometimes uninformed conversations.

## Target Users

### Primary Users

- **Economists**: Professionals who need to stay updated on the latest economic trends and analyses.
- **Political Analysts**: Individuals who analyze and discuss political developments and policies.
- **General Public**: Users who are interested in economic and political topics and want to engage in informed discussions.

### Secondary Users

- **Content Creators**: Individuals who create and share content related to economic and political topics.
- **Moderators**: Users responsible for ensuring the quality and appropriateness of content on the platform.

## Core Features

- **User Authentication**: Secure login and registration for members.
- **Article Management**: Users can create, edit, and delete their own articles.
- **Commenting System**: Users can comment on articles to engage in discussions.
- **File and Image Attachments**: Support for uploading and attaching files and images to articles.
- **Moderation Tools**: Administrators can manage and moderate content to ensure a positive and productive environment.

## Success Metrics

- **User Engagement**: Number of active users and the frequency of their interactions with the platform.
- **Content Quality**: The quality and relevance of the content posted on the platform.
- **User Satisfaction**: Feedback from users regarding their experience on the platform.
