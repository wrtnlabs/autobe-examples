# E-commerce Shopping Mall Platform Overview

## Service Vision

The vision of the e-commerce shopping mall platform is to create a seamless and user-friendly online shopping experience that connects buyers and sellers in a secure and efficient manner. The mission is to provide a comprehensive and scalable platform that meets the needs of both customers and sellers, while also driving revenue and growth for the business.

## Problem Statement

The problem that the e-commerce shopping mall platform aims to solve is the lack of a seamless and user-friendly online shopping experience. Many existing platforms are either too complex, lack key features, or have poor performance, leading to a negative user experience and low customer satisfaction. The platform aims to address these issues by providing a comprehensive and scalable solution that meets the needs of both customers and sellers.

## Target Audience

The primary target audience for the e-commerce shopping mall platform is customers who are looking for a seamless and user-friendly online shopping experience. The secondary target audience includes sellers who are looking to reach a wider audience and drive sales through the platform.

## Key Features

The key features of the e-commerce shopping mall platform include:

- User registration and login with address management
- Product catalog with categories and search
- Product variants (SKU) with different colors, sizes, options
- Shopping cart and wishlist
- Order placement and payment processing
- Order tracking and shipping status updates
- Product reviews and ratings
- Seller accounts to manage their products
- Inventory management per SKU
- Order history and cancellation/refund requests
- Admin dashboard for order and product management

## Business Model

The business model of the e-commerce shopping mall platform is based on a commission-based revenue stream. The platform will charge a commission on each successful transaction, with the revenue being split between the platform and the sellers. The platform will also offer premium features and services to sellers, such as advanced analytics and marketing tools, which will be available for a subscription fee.

## Success Metrics

The success metrics for the e-commerce shopping mall platform include:

- Number of active users
- Number of transactions
- Average order value
- Customer satisfaction score
- Seller satisfaction score
- Platform revenue
- Net profit margin

## Implementation Timeline

The implementation timeline for the e-commerce shopping mall platform includes the following milestones:

- Phase 1: Planning and Design (4 weeks)
- Phase 2: Development (8 weeks)
- Phase 3: Testing and Quality Assurance (4 weeks)
- Phase 4: Launch and Marketing (4 weeks)
