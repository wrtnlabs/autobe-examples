# User Stories for E-commerce Shopping Mall Platform

## User Personas

### Customer

- **Description**: Authenticated users who can browse products, add items to cart, place orders, and manage their profiles.
- **Goals**: Find and purchase desired products, track orders, leave reviews.
- **Pain Points**: Complex checkout process, limited product options, poor customer support.

### Seller

- **Description**: Authenticated users who can list products, manage inventory, and process orders.
- **Goals**: Increase sales, manage inventory, process orders efficiently.
- **Pain Points**: High transaction fees, limited marketing tools, complex inventory management.

### Admin

- **Description**: System administrators with elevated permissions who can manage users, products, and orders.
- **Goals**: Ensure platform security, manage user accounts, oversee order processing.
- **Pain Points**: Insufficient tools for fraud detection, lack of real-time analytics, complex user management.

## User Scenarios

### Customer

1. **Browsing Products**: Customers can browse products by category, search for specific items, and view product details.
2. **Adding to Cart**: Customers can add products to their cart, adjust quantities, and proceed to checkout.
3. **Placing Orders**: Customers can place orders, select payment methods, and view order confirmation.
4. **Tracking Orders**: Customers can track order status, view shipping details, and receive delivery notifications.
5. **Leaving Reviews**: Customers can leave reviews and ratings for purchased products.

### Seller

1. **Listing Products**: Sellers can list products, set prices, and manage product details.
2. **Managing Inventory**: Sellers can track inventory levels, receive low stock alerts, and update inventory.
3. **Processing Orders**: Sellers can process orders, update order status, and manage shipping details.

### Admin

1. **Managing Users**: Admins can manage user accounts, reset passwords, and deactivate accounts.
2. **Managing Products**: Admins can approve product listings, remove inappropriate content, and manage product categories.
3. **Managing Orders**: Admins can oversee order processing, resolve disputes, and manage refunds.
