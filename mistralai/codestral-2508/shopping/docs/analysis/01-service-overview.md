# Service Overview for E-commerce Shopping Mall Platform

## Executive Summary

This document provides a high-level overview of the e-commerce shopping mall platform, including its purpose, target audience, and key features. It serves as a foundation for understanding the project's vision and requirements before moving on to detailed user flow documentation.

## Problem Statement

The e-commerce shopping mall platform aims to solve the problem of providing a seamless and user-friendly online shopping experience. It addresses the need for a comprehensive platform that supports user registration and login, product catalog with categories and search, product variants with different colors, sizes, and options, shopping cart and wishlist, order placement and payment processing, order tracking and shipping status updates, product reviews and ratings, seller accounts to manage their products, inventory management per SKU, order history and cancellation/refund requests, and an admin dashboard for order and product management.

## Target Users

The primary users of the platform are customers who will browse products, add items to cart, place orders, and manage their profiles. Secondary users include sellers who will list products, manage inventory, and process orders, and admins who will manage users, products, and orders.

## Core Features

The core features of the platform include:

- User registration and login with address management.
- Product catalog with categories and search.
- Product variants (SKU) with different colors, sizes, and options.
- Shopping cart and wishlist.
- Order placement and payment processing.
- Order tracking and shipping status updates.
- Product reviews and ratings.
- Seller accounts to manage their products.
- Inventory management per SKU.
- Order history and cancellation/refund requests.
- Admin dashboard for order and product management.

## Success Metrics

The success metrics for the platform include:

- User acquisition and retention rates.
- Conversion rates for product purchases.
- Average order value and customer lifetime value.
- Customer satisfaction and net promoter score.
- Platform performance and uptime.

## Implementation Timeline

The implementation timeline for the platform includes the following milestones:

- **Phase 1**: Project initiation and planning (1 month).
- **Phase 2**: User interface and user experience design (2 months).
- **Phase 3**: Development of core features (3 months).
- **Phase 4**: Testing and quality assurance (1 month).
- **Phase 5**: Deployment and launch (1 month).

The total implementation timeline is estimated to be 8 months.
